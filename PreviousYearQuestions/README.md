### Syllabus & Curriculum Evolution Notes

1. **New OBE Curriculum (2023 – Present):**
   - **Course Title:** *Legal Issues, Industrial and Operational Management*
   - **Full Marks:** 60 | **Time:** 3 Hours
   - **Structure:** Explicitly mapped to Course Outcomes (**CO1**, **CO2**, **CO3**).
   - **Key Focus Areas:**
     - **Legal & Contract Issues (CO1):** Bangladesh Labor Code 2006 (child labor, workplace safety, trade unions, dispute resolution, collective bargaining) and Contract Act (agreements, valid/void contracts, ECE project bidding, termination of contracts).
     - **Industrial Management (CO2):** Management functions, administrative principles, managerial skills, employee motivation (Herzberg two-factor, Vroom expectancy, Maslow), performance management & appraisal problems.
     - **Operational Management & Inventory (CO3):** Continuous review systems, EOQ, Reorder Point ($R$), Safety Stock with $Z$-values, cycle service levels, and inventory holding cost analysis.

2. **Legacy Curriculum (2020 – 2021):**
   - **Course Title:** *Industrial Management & Accountancy*
   - **Full Marks:** 72 | **Time:** 3 Hours
   - **Structure:** Non-CO mapped format.
   - **Key Focus Areas:**
     - **Section A:** Core principles of management, scientific management, HR management, motivation theories, and inventory definitions.
     - **Section B:** Financial & Cost Accounting (Debit/Credit rules, Journal, Ledger, Cost Sheets, Break-Even Point / CVP analysis, and full Financial Statements including Trial Balance, Trading Account, P&L Account, and Balance Sheet).

### Digitization Standards Applied
- **Verbatim Accuracy:** 100% word-for-word fidelity with original exam papers.
- **LaTeX Math:** All formulas, variables, monetary amounts, and quantities are typeset using standard LaTeX syntax ($...$).
- **Tabular Layout:** Questions are organized into structured GitHub Flavored Markdown (GFM) tables with separate rows for sub-questions.
- **Cleanliness:** Handwritten marks, circled numbers, and margin calculations made by students on original exam scripts were eliminated.
- **Typo Annotations:** Editorial notes (`*(Note: ...)*`) are included where obvious typographical or grammatical errors existed in the printed question papers.
