## What is Law?

Law is a system of rules that govern a society with the intention of maintaining social order, upholding justice, and preventing harm to individuals’ property.

---

<!-- Slide 5 -->
## What is Industrial Law?

Industrial law is used to denote laws passed for the purpose of regulating the conditions under which work is carried out in factories and other establishments and the relationship between employers and employees.

---

<!-- Slide 6 -->
## What is Labor Law?

Labor law is designed to control and govern the continuous process by which workers and employers decide the terms and conditions of employment. It is a legal framework that governs the rights and responsibilities of employers and employees in the workplace.

---

<!-- Slide 7 -->
## Difference between Industrial Law and Labor Law

| Industrial Law | Labor Law |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. Industrial law is used to denote laws passed for the purpose of regulating the **conditions under which work is carried on factories and other establishments** | 1. Labor law is a set of legal forms relating to the employment relationship which **defines the rights and obligations of employees and employers** |
| 2. Industrial law is a law which is related to **Industry.** | 2. Labor law is a law which is related to **workers, employers and trade union etc.** |
| 3. The **scope** of industrial Law relating to different **industries.** | 3. The **scope** of labor law is relating to **industrial relations ordinance, payment of wages and workmen’s compensation etc.** |
| 4. It is important for proper rules and regulations of **industry.** | 4. It is important for **workers and employers in industry.** |
| 5. It can include a **wide range of legal topics** from employment law to environment concern contract, industrial relations. | 5. It includes the **benefits** of worker, conditions of leave, social security for workers. |
