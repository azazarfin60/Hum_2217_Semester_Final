> [!tip] Memory Hook (Sec 20–27 Notice & Compensation Key)
> Memorize key notice periods and severance rules across separation modes:
> - **Notice Days:**
>   - **`7d`:** Written show-cause explanation for alleged misconduct [`Sec 24(1)(b)`].
>   - **`30d`:** Written notice for retrenchment [`Sec 20(2)(a)`]. (Also for monthly temporary workers under `Sec 26` and `Sec 27`).
>   - **`60d / 30d`:** Resignation notice by worker (`60d` permanent, `30d` temporary monthly) [`Sec 27`].
>   - **`120d / 60d`:** Employer simple termination (`120d` permanent monthly, `60d` other permanent) [`Sec 26`].
> - **The "30 Days' Wages / Year" Rule:**
>   - Retrenchment [`Sec 20`], Discharge [`Sec 22`], and Employer Termination [`Sec 26`] share the same rule. They all pay **30 days' wages per completed year of service** (or gratuity).
>   - Resignation [`Sec 27`] pays **14 days' wages/year** for 5 to 10 years of service. It pays **30 days' wages/year** for 10 or more years.

## L09.1 · Retrenchment and Separation Modes Comparison

*Never asked in past finals. Noted in Raidah §15 as a key termination topic.*

### L09.1 · Model Exam Question 1 · 04 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter II) · Target: Part (b) or (c)*

**Question.** Define retrenchment under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. Explain the statutory conditions, compensation rules, and the principle of seniority under Section 20.

**Answer.**

Under **Section 2(54)** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, retrenchment means terminating workers due to redundancy. It is not an act of punishment.

#### Statutory Requirements under Section 20

If a worker completes at least **1 year of continuous service**, the employer must fulfill these rules before retrenchment:

1. **Written Notice [`Sec 20(2)(a)`]:**  
   The employer must serve **1 month written notice** stating the reasons for retrenchment. Alternatively, the employer may pay wages in lieu of notice.

2. **Official Notice Copies [`Sec 20(2)(b)`]:**  
   The employer must send copies of the notice to the Chief Inspector. A copy also goes to the Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA).

3. **Statutory Compensation [`Sec 20(2)(c)`]:**  
   The worker receives **30 days' wages per completed year of service**, or gratuity, whichever is higher.

4. **Principle of Seniority (LIFO Rule) [`Sec 20(4)`]:**  
   The employer must follow the "Last In, First Out" (LIFO) rule. The employer must retrench the latest hire in that category. This applies unless an agreement states otherwise.

5. **Re-employment Preference [`Sec 21`]:**  
   If the employer hires workers in that category within **1 year**, retrenched workers must be notified. Those who apply receive priority based on their previous seniority.

### L09.1 · Model Exam Question 2 · 05 Marks

*Suggested Master Question · Target: Part (b) or (c)*

**Question.** Distinguish among retrenchment, discharge, dismissal, termination by notice, and resignation under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. Present a comparison matrix.

**Answer.**

The **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (BLC 2006)** establishes five distinct separation modes under Chapter II:

#### Master Separation Comparison Matrix

| Separation Mode | BLC Section | Primary Cause or Ground | Mandatory Notice Period | Statutory Compensation Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retrenchment** | **Sec 20** | Redundancy of post or surplus labor force. | 1 month written notice (or wages in lieu). | 30 days wages per completed year of service, or gratuity. |
| **Discharge** | **Sec 22** | Physical/mental incapacity or certified ill-health. | No statutory notice required. | 30 days wages per completed year (if service $\ge 1$ year). |
| **Dismissal** | **Sec 23–24** | Proven misconduct or criminal conviction. | No notice (requires formal domestic inquiry). | Zero compensation for theft/riot; 15 days/yr for other misconduct. |
| **Termination by Notice** | **Sec 26** | Employer termination without alleging fault. | 120 days (monthly permanent); 60 days (other permanent). | 30 days wages per completed year of service, or gratuity. |
| **Resignation** | **Sec 27** | Voluntary separation initiated by the worker. | 60 days written notice (permanent worker). | 14 days/yr (5 to $< 10$ yrs); 30 days/yr ($\ge 10$ yrs service). |

#### Key Legal Distinctions

1. **Punitive vs. Non-Punitive:** Dismissal is strictly punitive and requires a formal inquiry. Retrenchment, discharge, and simple termination carry no disciplinary stigma.
2. **Medical Grounds:** Discharge applies solely to medical unfitness certified by a registered physician.
3. **Notice Periods:** Termination under Section 26 requires the longest notice (120 days). Retrenchment requires 30 days, while dismissal requires none.

## L09.2 · Discharge from Service

*Never asked in past finals. Core statutory termination provision.*

### L09.2 · Model Exam Question 3 · 03 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter II) · Target: Part (a) or (b)*

**Question.** Explain the grounds and legal consequences of discharge from service under Section 22 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. How does discharge differ from dismissal?

**Answer.**

Under **Section 22** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, discharge is a non-punitive termination based on physical disability:

1. **Lawful Grounds [`Sec 22(1)`]:**  
   A worker may be discharged solely for physical incapacity, mental incapacity, or continued ill-health. A registered doctor must certify the medical condition.

2. **Statutory Compensation [`Sec 22(2)`]:**  
   If the worker completed at least **1 year of continuous service**, the employer must pay:
   - Compensation equal to **30 days' wages for each completed year of service**, or
   - Gratuity, whichever is higher.

3. **Discharge vs. Dismissal:**  
   - Discharge is not a punishment. It involves no misconduct, no disciplinary inquiry, and no personal blame.
   - Dismissal under Section 23 punishes moral or behavioral wrongdoing. It damages worker reputation and can forfeit severance pay.

## L09.3 · Dismissal for Misconduct and Inquiry Procedure

*Never asked in past finals. Prescribed in Raidah §15.*

### L09.3 · Model Exam Question 4 · 04 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter II) · Target: Part (b) or (c)*

**Question.** What acts constitute misconduct under Section 23 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006? Detail the mandatory inquiry procedure prescribed under Section 24.

**Answer.**

#### 1. Acts of Misconduct [`Sec 23(4)`]

Under **Section 23(4)** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, misconduct includes:
- Willful disobedience to lawful orders.
- Theft, misappropriation, fraud, or dishonesty regarding employer property.
- Taking or offering bribes.
- Habitual absence without leave, or unexcused absence exceeding 10 consecutive days.
- Habitual late attendance or negligence.
- Riotous behavior, arson, disorderliness, or intentional damage to factory records.

#### 2. Statutory Inquiry Procedure [`Sec 24`]

Under **Section 24**, no punishment of dismissal can be imposed without following this 5-step procedure:

```text
Step 1: Written Charge-Sheet (Sec 24(1)(a))
    │
    ▼
Step 2: 7-Day Explanation Period (Sec 24(1)(b))
    │
    ▼
Step 3: Personal Hearing & Representation (Sec 24(1)(c), (4))
    │
    ▼
Step 4: Domestic Inquiry Committee (Sec 24(1)(d))
    │   (Equal reps of employer and worker; concluded within 60 days)
    ▼
Step 5: Final Approval by Employer / Manager (Sec 24(1)(e))
```

1. **Written Allegation [`Sec 24(1)(a)`]:** The employer must record the specific charge in writing.
2. **Explanation Notice [`Sec 24(1)(b)`]:** The worker must receive a copy of the charge-sheet. The worker must receive at least **7 days** to submit a written explanation.
3. **Personal Hearing [`Sec 24(1)(c)`]:** The accused worker must receive an opportunity of being heard. A nominated co-worker may assist the accused during the proceedings [`Sec 24(4)`].
4. **Bipartite Inquiry Committee [`Sec 24(1)(d)`]:** An inquiry committee must investigate the charges. The committee consists of equal numbers of employer and worker representatives. It must conclude its inquiry within **60 days**.
5. **Managerial Approval [`Sec 24(1)(e)`]:** The employer or factory manager must formally approve the dismissal order.

#### 3. Suspension Pending Inquiry [`Sec 24(2)`]

The employer may suspend the worker pending inquiry for up to **60 days**. During suspension, the employer pays a subsistence allowance equal to **half basic wages**. The employer also pays full medical and housing allowances. If acquitted, the worker receives full back wages.

## L09.4 · Termination by Notice by Employer

*Never asked in past finals. Core statutory termination provision.*

### L09.4 · Model Exam Question 5 · 03 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter II) · Target: Part (a) or (b)*

**Question.** What are the notice periods and compensation rules for employer termination under Section 26 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006?

**Answer.**

**Section 26** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006** allows an employer to terminate employment without citing misconduct or redundancy:

#### Statutory Notice Periods [`Sec 26(1), (2)`]

| Worker Category | Employment Type | Required Written Notice Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Permanent Worker** | Monthly-rated | **120 days** notice in writing. |
| **Permanent Worker** | Other (daily or piece-rated) | **60 days** notice in writing. |
| **Temporary Worker** | Monthly-rated | **30 days** notice in writing. |
| **Temporary Worker** | Other (daily or piece-rated) | **14 days** notice in writing. |

#### Compensation and Wages in Lieu

1. **Wages in Lieu of Notice [`Sec 26(3)`]:**  
   The employer may terminate the worker immediately by paying wages equivalent to the required notice period.

2. **Service Compensation [`Sec 26(4)`]:**  
   A terminated permanent worker receives **30 days' wages for each completed year of service**. Alternatively, they receive gratuity if higher.

3. **Cumulative Benefits:**  
   This compensation is payable in addition to earned leave encashment and provident fund dues.

## L09.5 · Resignation by Worker and Applied Case Study

*Never asked in past finals. Flagged in Raidah §15 as a prospective 10-Mark Applied Case Study topic.*

### L09.5 · Model Exam Question 6 · 03 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter II) · Target: Part (a) or (b)*

**Question.** Explain the legal rules governing resignation by a worker under Section 27 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. What compensation is payable for different lengths of service?

**Answer.**

Under **Section 27** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, a worker may resign voluntarily:

1. **Notice Requirements [`Sec 27(1), (2)`]:**  
   - A permanent worker must give **60 days written notice** to the employer.
   - A temporary monthly-rated worker must give **30 days notice**.
   - Other temporary workers must give **14 days notice**.
   - A worker may resign immediately by paying wages in lieu of the notice period [`Sec 27(3)`].

2. **Statutory Service Benefits for Permanent Workers [`Sec 27(4)`]:**  
   - **5 to 10 years of service:** 14 days' wages for each completed year of service.
   - **10 or more years of service:** 30 days' wages for each completed year of service. The worker receives gratuity if higher.
   - **Under 5 years of service:** No service compensation is payable. The worker receives only unpaid wages and accrued leave benefits.

3. **Deemed Resignation for Unexcused Absence [`Sec 27(3A)`]:**  
   A worker absent without leave for over 10 consecutive days receives a 10-day notice to explain and rejoin. If the worker fails to respond, the employer issues a final 7-day defense notice. Continued failure results in deemed release from service.

### L09.5 · Model Case Study · 10 Marks

*High-Yield Applied Scenario · Target: Full 10-Mark Question (Section A)*

**Case Scenario.**  
*Apex Telecommunications Ltd. operates an assembly and testing facility in Rajshahi. Due to automation, the company cuts 15 technician jobs. Rafiq has served 6 years as a Senior Assembly Technician. In contrast, Tanvir joined only 14 months ago for the same role. Management abruptly dismisses Rafiq without notice, citing cost reduction, while retaining Tanvir. On the same day, a supervisor accuses worker Karim of theft. The supervisor dismisses Karim on the spot without a charge-sheet or inquiry. Finally, Farhana, a software technician, resigns voluntarily after 8 continuous years of service.*

**Questions:**  
(i) Evaluate the legality of Rafiq's termination. Calculate the notice period and statutory compensation owed to him under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. **[03 Marks]**  
(ii) Assess whether Karim's summary dismissal is lawful. State the mandatory legal procedure the management must follow. **[03 Marks]**  
(iii) Determine Farhana's statutory entitlements upon resignation after 8 years of service. Compare her compensation with termination under Section 26. **[04 Marks]**

**Answer.**

#### Part (i): Legality of Rafiq's Termination and Statutory Compensation [03 Marks]

1. **Facts / Issue of the Case:**  
   Management fired Rafiq without notice due to automation. Yet, the company retained junior technician Tanvir in the same role.

2. **Applicable Law & Section:**  
   **Section 20 (Retrenchment)** and **Section 20(4) (LIFO Rule)** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**.

3. **Legal Analysis:**  
   - Downsizing due to automation constitutes redundancy under Section 2(54). The separation must follow retrenchment rules under Section 20.
   - Under Section 20(4), the employer must follow the Last In, First Out (LIFO) rule within the same post. Tanvir served only 14 months, while Rafiq served 6 years. Retrenching senior Rafiq while retaining junior Tanvir violates Section 20(4).
   - Under Section 20(2)(a), the employer failed to provide 1 month written notice or wages in lieu.
   - Statutory compensation payable under Section 20(2)(c) is 30 days wages per completed year of service:
     $$
     \text{Service Compensation} = 6 \text{ completed years} \times 30 \text{ days wages} = 180 \text{ days wages}
     $$
   - Rafiq also receives 1 month (30 days) wages in lieu of notice. His total compensation equals 210 days' wages.

4. **Direct Conclusion:**  
   Rafiq's immediate dismissal is unlawful. Apex Telecommunications must retrench junior technician Tanvir first. If the company retrenches Rafiq later, it must pay 1 month notice wages. It must also pay 180 days' wages as severance.

---

#### Part (ii): Lawfulness of Karim's Summary Dismissal [03 Marks]

1. **Facts / Issue of the Case:**  
   A supervisor fired Karim on the spot for alleged theft. The supervisor gave him no charge-sheet and conducted no inquiry.

2. **Applicable Law & Section:**  
   **Section 23(4)(b) (Theft as Misconduct)** and **Section 24 (Procedure of Punishment)** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**.

3. **Legal Analysis:**  
   - Theft is an act of misconduct under Section 23(4)(b).
   - Under Section 24(1), dismissal requires strict adherence to natural justice:
     1. Issue a formal written charge-sheet stating the allegation [`Sec 24(1)(a)`].
     2. Give Karim at least 7 days to submit a written explanation [`Sec 24(1)(b)`].
     3. Provide an opportunity for a personal hearing [`Sec 24(1)(c)`].
     4. Form an inquiry committee with equal worker and employer members [`Sec 24(1)(d)`]. The committee must conclude within 60 days.
     5. Obtain written approval from the employer or factory manager [`Sec 24(1)(e)`].
   - An on-the-spot oral dismissal by a supervisor bypasses all statutory safeguards.

4. **Direct Conclusion:**  
   Karim's summary dismissal is null and void. The company must reinstate him. Alternatively, it can suspend him with a subsistence allowance pending a proper inquiry.

---

#### Part (iii): Farhana's Resignation Entitlements vs. Section 26 Termination [04 Marks]

1. **Facts / Issue of the Case:**  
   Farhana resigned voluntarily after 8 years of continuous service. Her compensation must be evaluated and compared with employer termination.

2. **Applicable Law & Section:**  
   **Section 27(1), (4)(a) (Resignation)** and **Section 26(1)(a), (4) (Termination by Employer)** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**.

3. **Legal Analysis:**  
   - Under Section 27(1), Farhana must provide 60 days written notice to the company.
   - Under Section 27(4)(a), a permanent worker resigning between 5 and 10 continuous years receives statutory compensation. The rate is **14 days' wages for each completed year of service**:
     $$
     \text{Resignation Compensation} = 8 \text{ completed years} \times 14 \text{ days wages} = 112 \text{ days wages}
     $$
   - In contrast, if the employer terminated her under Section 26 (simple termination):
     - The employer would have to give 120 days written notice (or wages in lieu).
     - Compensation under Section 26(4) would be **30 days' wages per completed year**:
     $$
     \text{Section 26 Compensation} = 8 \text{ completed years} \times 30 \text{ days wages} = 240 \text{ days wages}
     $$

4. **Direct Conclusion:**  
   Upon giving 60 days' notice, Farhana receives **112 days' wages** under Section 27(4)(a). She also receives accrued leave encashment and provident fund balances. This is far less than the 240 days' wages paid under employer termination.
