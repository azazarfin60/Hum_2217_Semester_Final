> - **$1$ box per $150$ workers:** First-aid box under trained first-aider [`Sec 89(2)`].

## L08.1 · First-Aid Appliances and Comprehensive Welfare Measures

*Tested inside Legal CT-2 Q.1. Prescribed as "Full Topic Important" by Shoaib Sir [05:30–05:55] and Raidah §15.*

### L08.1 · Model Exam Question 1 · 04 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (b) or (c)*

**Question.** State the statutory provisions regarding first-aid appliances under Section 89 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. When is a factory required to provide a sick room and dispensary?

**Answer.**

Under **Section 89** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, every establishment must maintain first-aid equipment. This ensures immediate treatment for workplace injuries:

1. **Accessibility and Equipment [`Sec 89(1)`]:**  
   Every establishment must provide first-aid boxes or cupboards. They must be equipped with the prescribed contents. They must remain readily accessible during all working hours.

2. **Ratio of First-Aid Boxes [`Sec 89(2)`]:**  
   The employer must provide at least one first-aid box for every **150 workers**.

3. **Trained Person in Charge [`Sec 89(3)`]:**  
   Each first-aid box must stay under a responsible person trained in first aid. This person must remain available during all working hours.

4. **Notice and Identification Badge [`Sec 89(4)`]:**  
   A notice stating the first-aider's name must hang in every workroom. The designated handler must wear an identification badge during work hours.

5. **Dispensary and Sick Room Requirement [`Sec 89(5)`]:**  
   A sick room with an attached dispensary is mandatory in establishments employing **300 or more workers**. It must meet these conditions:
   - Built to the prescribed room dimensions.
   - Equipped with necessary medical appliances and medicines.
   - Staffed by qualified medical doctors and nursing staff.

6. **Medical Centers and Welfare Officers (2013 Amendment):**  
   - Establishments with **500 or more workers** must appoint a certified welfare officer [`Sec 89(8)`].
   - Establishments with **5,000 or more workers** must establish and run a permanent medical center [`Sec 89(6)`].

### L08.1 · Model Exam Question 2 · 05 Marks

*Suggested Comprehensive Question · Target: Part (b) or (c)*

**Question.** Summarize the key welfare measures provided under Chapter VIII of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. Present the statutory threshold requirements in a summary table.

**Answer.**

**Chapter VIII (Sections 89 to 99)** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006** mandates welfare amenities for workers. These provisions prevent occupational fatigue and support working families.

#### Statutory Threshold Summary Table

| Section | Welfare Measure | Minimum Worker Threshold | Key Statutory Mandate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sec 89(2)** | First-Aid Box | $\ge 1$ box per $150$ workers | Trained person with badge available during working hours. |
| **Sec 89(5)** | Dispensary & Sick Room | $\ge 300$ workers | Prescribed size, equipped, staffed by medical and nursing personnel. |
| **Sec 89(8)** | Welfare Officer | $\ge 500$ workers | Appointed to oversee worker welfare (2013 amendment). |
| **Sec 89(6)** | Permanent Medical Center | $\ge 5,000$ workers | Fully functional medical facility run by employer (2013 amendment). |
| **Sec 90** | Safety Record Book & Board | $> 25$ workers | Compulsory record book and prominent safety information board. |
| **Sec 90A** | Safety Committee | $\ge 50$ workers | Joint committee of employer and worker representatives (2013 amendment). |
| **Sec 91** | Washing Facilities | All establishments | Adequate, clean, separate, and screened facilities for male and female workers. |
| **Sec 92** | Canteen | $> 100$ workers | Subsidized non-profit food managed with worker representation. |
| **Sec 93** | Rest Rooms & Lunch Rooms | $> 50$ workers | Sufficient light, ventilation, and drinking water; separate room if $> 25$ female workers. |
| **Sec 94** | Rooms for Children (Crèche) | $\ge 40$ female workers | For children under 6 years; trained woman attendant, cots, toys, playground. |
| **Sec 99** | Compulsory Group Insurance | $\ge 100$ permanent workers | Group life/accident coverage; claims paid directly to dependents within 120 days. |

#### Operational Summary

1. **Health Care:** First-aid boxes provide immediate relief on the shop floor. Sick rooms handle severe ailments under professional medical care.
2. **Hygiene:** Screened washrooms ensure personal privacy and dignity for both male and female staff.
3. **Nutrition and Rest:** Canteens and dining rooms prevent workers from eating in hazardous production areas.
4. **Family Support:** Crèches enable nursing mothers to work without compromising child safety and care.

## L08.2 · Safety Record Book and Safety Board

*Tested inside Legal CT-2 Q.1. Prescribed in Raidah §15.*

### L08.2 · Model Exam Question 3 · 02 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (a)*

**Question.** What are the rules for safety records, boards, and committees under Sections 90 and 90A of BLC 2006?

**Answer.**

Under the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**:

1. **Safety Record Book and Board [`Sec 90`]:**  
   Every establishment employing **more than 25 workers** must maintain a safety record book. The employer must also display a safety information board. This board alerts workers to hazards and safety rules.

2. **Constitution of Safety Committee [`Sec 90A`]:**  
   Under the 2013 Amendment, every factory employing **50 or more workers** must form a safety committee. The committee includes equal members from workers and management. It monitors workplace hazards and enforces safety standards.

## L08.3 · Washing Facilities

*Tested inside Legal CT-2 Q.1. Prescribed in Raidah §15.*

### L08.3 · Model Exam Question 4 · 02 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (a)*

**Question.** What are the legal requirements regarding washing facilities under Section 91 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006?

**Answer.**

Under **Section 91** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, every employer must provide adequate washing amenities:

1. **Adequate and Clean Facilities [`Sec 91(1)(a), (c)`]:**  
   Employers must provide sufficient and suitable washing facilities. They must be conveniently accessible to workers and kept clean at all times.

2. **Gender Separation and Screening [`Sec 91(1)(b)`]:**  
   The facilities must be separate for male and female workers. They must be adequately screened to protect worker privacy.

3. **Regulatory Standards [`Sec 91(2)`]:**  
   The Government may prescribe minimum washing standards for specific industries or chemical processes.

## L08.4 · Canteens

*Tested inside Legal CT-2 Q.1. Explicitly named by Shoaib Sir [05:30–05:55].*

### L08.4 · Model Exam Question 5 · 03 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (a) or (b)*

**Question.** Explain the statutory rules governing canteens under Section 92 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006.

**Answer.**

Under **Section 92** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, canteen facilities are regulated as follows:

1. **Mandatory Threshold [`Sec 92(1)`]:**  
   Every establishment employing **more than 100 workers** must provide an adequate number of canteens.

2. **Managing Committee [`Sec 92(2)(b)`]:**  
   The canteen must be run by a managing committee. This committee must include elected or nominated worker representatives.

3. **Food and Subsidized Charges [`Sec 92(3)`]:**  
   The managing committee decides the foodstuffs to be served. It also fixes food charges, operating on a non-profit and subsidized basis.

4. **Construction Standards [`Sec 92(2)(a)`]:**  
   Government rules prescribe structural standards, accommodation space, and furniture. They also regulate kitchen hygiene and ventilation for the canteen building.

## L08.5 · Shelters, Rest Rooms, and Lunch Rooms

*Tested inside Legal CT-2 Q.1. Explicitly named by Shoaib Sir [05:30–05:55].*

### L08.5 · Model Exam Question 6 · 03 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (a) or (b)*

**Question.** What does Section 93 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 provide regarding shelters, rest rooms, and lunch rooms?

**Answer.**

Under **Section 93** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, employers must provide dedicated rest facilities:

1. **Employment Threshold [`Sec 93(1)`]:**  
   Every establishment with **more than 50 workers** must provide adequate shelters or rest rooms. It must also provide a suitable lunch room with drinking water.

2. **Prohibition of Eating in Workrooms [`Sec 93(1)`]:**  
   Workers must not eat any meal inside active workrooms when a lunch room exists. A canteen maintained under Section 92 fulfills this rest requirement.

3. **Physical Conditions [`Sec 93(2)`]:**  
   All rest rooms and lunch rooms must have sufficient light and ventilation. They must remain clean and maintain a comfortable temperature.

4. **Gender-Specific Rules [`Sec 93(3)`]:**  
   - If female workers exceed **25**, the employer must provide a separate rest room for women.
   - If female workers are **under 25**, separate screened spaces within the common room are sufficient.

## L08.6 · Rooms for Children (Crèches)

*Tested inside Legal CT-2 Q.1. Prescribed in Raidah §15.*

### L08.6 · Model Exam Question 7 · 04 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (b) or (c)*

**Question.** Detail the statutory requirements for providing rooms for children (crèches) under Section 94 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006.

**Answer.**

Under **Section 94** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006**, employers must provide childcare facilities for working mothers:

1. **Threshold and Beneficiaries [`Sec 94(1)`]:**  
   Mandatory in every establishment employing **40 or more female workers**. The room accommodates children **under 6 years of age**.

2. **Location and Building Standards [`Sec 94(3), (4)`]:**  
   - Must be easily accessible to mothers during nursing intervals.
   - Must not be situated near obnoxious fumes, heavy dust, toxic odors, or noisy industrial machinery.
   - Walls and roof must use solid, heat-resisting, and waterproof construction materials.

3. **Dimensions and Space Standards [`Sec 94(5)`]:**  
   - Room height must be at least **$360\text{ cm}$** from floor to ceiling.
   - Floor area must be at least **$6000\text{ sq. cm}$** per child. *(Note: The English translation in the original Act prints $600\text{ sq. cm}$, which is an acknowledged typo for $6000\text{ sq. cm}$).*

4. **Equipment and Facilities [`Sec 94(7)`]:**  
   - At least one suitable cot or cradle with clean bedding for each child.
   - At least one chair for each mother while feeding or attending to her infant.
   - A sufficient supply of suitable toys for older children.

5. **Supervision [`Sec 94(2)`]:**  
   A woman trained or experienced in child care must supervise the room.

6. **Open-Air Playground [`Sec 94(8)`]:**  
   A fenced, shady open-air playground must be provided for older children. The Chief Inspector may grant a written exemption only if space is unavailable.

## L08.7 · Compulsory Group Insurance

*Never asked in past finals. Core statutory welfare provision.*

### L08.7 · Model Exam Question 8 · 02 Marks

*Suggested Topic (Chapter VIII) · Target: Part (a)*

**Question.** What does Section 99 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 stipulate regarding compulsory group insurance?

**Answer.**

Under **Section 99** of the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006** (as amended in 2013):

1. **Mandatory Threshold [`Sec 99(1)`]:**  
   Every establishment with at least **100 permanent workers** must introduce group insurance under existing insurance laws. *(Note: The original 2006 text stated 200 permanent workers, lowered to 100 in 2013).*

2. **Additional Benefit [`Sec 99(2)`]:**  
   Insurance compensation is paid in addition to all other terminal dues and benefits under the Act.

3. **Employer Duty on Death [`Sec 99(2)`]:**  
   If a worker dies, the employer must recover the insurance claim from the insurer. The employer must disburse the amount directly to the worker's legal dependents.

4. **Settlement Deadline [`Sec 99(2)`]:**  
   The insurer and employer must settle the claim jointly within **120 days** of raising it.
