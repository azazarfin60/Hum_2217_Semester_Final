# L03.2: Exceptions to "No Consideration, No Contract"

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · SI Sir · 🔴 Highest | **Read Time:** ~3 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** Prescribed Ch. 2 Core Target (4m theory + 6m 3-scenario applied problem)
> - **Scoring Rule:** State Sec 25 rule, cite all 7 exceptions with sections, and apply the 4 W-R-L-N conditions.

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **No Right of Action [Maxim]:** "Ex nudo pacto non oritur actio." *(Latin Legal Maxim)*
- *Plain meaning:* A bare agreement without consideration gives rise to no legal action.

> **Agreement Without Consideration [Sec 25]:** "An agreement made without consideration is void." *(Contract Act, 1872)*
- *Plain meaning:* Promises made without consideration have no binding legal force.

> **Agency Creation [Sec 185]:** "No consideration is necessary to create an agency." *(Contract Act, 1872)*
- *Plain meaning:* An agent is legally appointed even without receiving compensation or fees.

---

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### The Seven Statutory Exceptions to Section 25

Under Section 25 of the Contract Act 1872, an agreement without consideration is void. The Act recognizes seven exceptions:

1. **Natural Love & Affection [Sec 25(1)]:** Enforceable without consideration under four mandatory conditions (`W-R-L-N`):
   - In **writing** and **registered** under document registration law.
   - Arises from genuine **natural love and affection** between parties in **near relation**.
   - *Ex:* Father registers deed gifting Tk. 100,000 to son out of love.
   - *Negative Precedent:* In *Rajlukhy Dabee v. Bhootnath*, maintenance deed after bitter quarrels was void for lack of love.
2. **Past Voluntary Service [Sec 25(2)]:** Promise to compensate past voluntary service for promisor is valid.
   - *Ex:* A returns B's lost laptop; B's promise to pay Tk. 2,000 reward binds B.
3. **Time-Barred Debt [Sec 25(3)]:** Enforceable if in writing and signed by debtor or authorized agent.
   - *Ex:* Debtor signs letter promising Tk. 30,000 on expired 4-year debt.
4. **Completed Gifts [Sec 25 Expl. 1]:** Rule does not affect gifts already completed by actual delivery.
   - *Ex:* Delivered lab equipment cannot be demanded back for lack of consideration.
5. **Agency Creation [Sec 185]:** No consideration is needed to create an agency.
   - *Ex:* Appointing an unpaid agent to sell equipment binds the principal.
6. **Guarantee [Sec 127]:** Benefit to principal debtor serves as sufficient consideration for surety's promise. (Under Sec 63, remission also dispenses with consideration).
   - *Ex:* Creditor lends Tk. 500,000 to borrower because surety signs guarantee. Loan to borrower binds surety without separate fee.
7. **Charitable Subscription with Detriment:** Pledges bind if promisee incurs financial debt relying on them.
   - *Leading Case:* In *Kedarnath v. Gorie Mahomed*, subscriber was liable because committee contracted building debts relying on pledges.
   - *Contrast:* In *Abdul Aziz v. Masum Ali*, void because no liability arose before death.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: 7 Exceptions & Sec 25(1) Keys
> - **7 Exceptions (L-V-T-A-G-G-C):** **L**ove [25(1)], **V**oluntary service [25(2)], **T**ime-barred debt [25(3)], **A**gency [185], **G**ifts [25 Expl. 1], **G**uarantee [127], **C**harity.
> - **Sec 25(1) Keys (W-R-L-N):** **W**ritten, **R**egistered, **L**ove, **N**ear relation.

## 🎯 3. PYQ & Target Answer Key

### Model Exam Question 1 · Rule of Sec 25 & Seven Exceptions (04 Marks)
> **Question:** "An agreement made without consideration is void." Discuss this rule (*Ex nudo pacto non oritur actio*) under Section 25 and state all statutory exceptions.

**Answer Blueprint:**
- State the Latin maxim and Section 25 rule from §1.
- List all 7 statutory exceptions from §2 with exact sections (`Sec 25(1)`, `25(2)`, `25(3)`, `25 Expl. 1`, `185`, `127`, Judicial Precedent).
- Highlight `W-R-L-N` test of Section 25(1) and cite *Rajlukhy Dabee* for full 4 marks.

---

### Model Exam Question 2 · Applied 3-Scenario Problem (06 Marks)
> **Question:** Evaluate the validity of agreements in these three scenarios under Contract Act 1872:

#### Scenario (i) · Registered Land Gift to Brother (02 Marks)
1. **Facts:** Brother registers written deed gifting land to brother out of natural love without price.
2. **Law:** Section 25(1), Contract Act 1872.
3. **Analysis:** Satisfies all four elements: written, registered, natural love, and near blood relations.
4. **Conclusion:** Valid and legally enforceable contract.

#### Scenario (ii) · Reward for Found Drone (02 Marks)
1. **Facts:** Student voluntarily returns lost drone. Director signs note promising Tk. 10,000 reward.
2. **Law:** Section 25(2), Contract Act 1872.
3. **Analysis:** Voluntary past service performed for promisor supports subsequent promise.
4. **Conclusion:** Valid contract. Student can recover reward.

#### Scenario (iii) · Verbal Promise on Old Debt (02 Marks)
1. **Facts:** Firm director verbally promises over phone to pay Tk. 60,000 on an expired 4-year debt, then refuses.
2. **Law:** Section 25(3), Contract Act 1872 and Limitation Act 1908.
3. **Analysis:** Debts expire after 3 years. Section 25(3) strictly requires a written and signed promise.
4. **Conclusion:** Void and unenforceable. Creditor cannot recover money.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Sec 25(1) Requires All 4 Keys:** Writing and registration fail without genuine love (*Rajlukhy Dabee* quarrels proved lack of love).
> - **Oral Time-Barred Debt Promise Is Void:** Section 25(3) strictly requires writing and signature. Oral promises have zero legal effect.
> - **Charity Pledges Need Detriment:** Pledges bind only if the promisee incurred actual debt relying on them (*Kedarnath* rule).
