# L12.1: Unfair Labour Practices (Sections 195 and 196)

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · Shoaib Islam Sir · High Priority | **Read Time:** ~3 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2023 Q.4(b) [04m], 2023 Q.4(c) [04m], Shoaib Sir Target Qs
> - **Scoring Rule:** State exact section numbers (195, 196, 291) and statutory penalty tiers.

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

- **Unfair Labour Practice:** *"Any unfair act or omission that arises between an employer and an employee."* (SI Sir Notes Ch. 4)
  - *Plain meaning:* Unlawful actions attacking worker rights or abusing employer authority.
- **Yellow-Dog Contract:** An agreement forcing a worker to promise not to join a trade union. **`[Sec 195(a)]`**
  - *Plain meaning:* Illegal contract term barring union membership.
- **Go-Slow:** Deliberate slowing down of normal output by workers without stopping work entirely. **`[Sec 196(2)(e)]`** / **`[Sec 2(lviii)]`**
  - *Plain meaning:* Deliberately reducing output speed without a full strike.

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Employer Unfair Labour Practices (Section 195)

Section 195 of the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 lists 12 prohibited employer acts:

1. **Yellow-Dog Contracts [Sec 195(a)]:** Banning union membership in employment contracts.
2. **Refusal to Employ [Sec 195(b)]:** Refusing to hire or retain workers for union activity.
3. **Service Discrimination [Sec 195(c)]:** Discriminating in wages, promotion, or terms for union support.
4. **Retaliatory Dismissal [Sec 195(d)]:** Firing or threatening workers for union participation.
5. **Bribery / Inducement [Sec 195(e)]:** Offering money or perks to induce leaving union office.
6. **Coercing the CBA [Sec 195(f)]:** Forcing CBA officers to sign deals via threats or utility cuts.
7. **Rigging CBA Ballots [Sec 195(g)]:** Interfering with secret ballot elections under Section 202.
8. **Hiring Strike-Breakers [Sec 195(h)]:** Hiring replacement workers during lawful strikes without Arbitrator approval.
9. **Ignoring Committee [Sec 195(i)]:** Refusing to implement agreed Participation Committee advice.
10. **Refusing Communication [Sec 195(j)]:** Failing to answer official CBA dispute letters.
11. **Punitive Transfer [Sec 195(k)]:** Transferring President, General Secretary, Organizing Secretary, or Treasurer without consent (`Sec 187`).
12. **Illegal Lock-Out [Sec 195(l)]:** Starting or instigating an illegal lock-out.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: 4 Section 195 Clusters
> - **Rights (a-d):** Yellow-dog, refuse hire, discriminate, retaliatory firing.
> - **Structure (e, g, k):** Bribery, ballot rigging, transferring 4 key officers.
> - **Dialogue (f, i, j):** Coerce CBA, ignore committee, refuse letters.
> - **Action (h, l):** Strike-breakers, illegal lock-out.

### Worker and Union Unfair Labour Practices (Section 196)

Section 196 protects management, factory assets, and non-union staff:

1. **Working Hours Rule [Sec 196(1)]:** No union work during working hours without leave. Exception: CBA officers attending dispute talks after notice.
2. **Six Prohibited Worker Acts [Sec 196(2)]:**
   - **Membership Coercion [Sec 196(2)(a)]:** Threatening workers to join, stay, or quit.
   - **Bribery to Quit Office [Sec 196(2)(b)]:** Offering perks to induce leaving union office.
   - **Forcing Union Dues [Sec 196(2)(c)]:** Compelling dues via threats, assault, or utility cuts.
   - **Coercing Employer [Sec 196(2)(d)]:** Compelling pacts via assault, confinement, threats, or cutting utilities.
   - **Illegal Strike & Go-Slow [Sec 196(2)(e)]:** Starting or leading an illegal strike or go-slow.
   - **Gherao & Damage [Sec 196(2)(f)]:** Confinement of managers, blockades, or destroying property.
3. **Ballot Tampering [Sec 196(3)]:** Undue influence, threats, or bribery during CBA ballots.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: Section 196(2) Split (3 Inward + 3 Outward)
> - **3 Inward (Against Workers):** Threats (a), bribery to quit (b), forced dues (c).
> - **3 Outward (Against Enterprise):** Coercion (d), strike / go-slow (e), gherao / damage (f).

### Statutory Comparison & Penalties (Sec 195 vs. Sec 196 & Sec 291)

| Dimension | Employer ULPs (`Sec 195`) | Worker & Union ULPs (`Sec 196`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | 12 statutory prohibitions (`Sec 195(a)–(l)`) | Work-hour ban (`(1)`), 6 prohibited acts (`(2)`), ballot rule (`(3)`) |
| **Key Coercions** | Yellow-dog pacts, illegal lock-outs, strike-breakers | Gherao, utility cuts, go-slow, wildcat strikes, duress |
| **Officer Protection** | Bans transfer of President, Secretary, Org. Sec, Treasurer | Bans ballot rigging, bribes, and membership coercion |
| **Penalty [Sec 291]** | Up to **2 years prison**, fine up to **10,000 Tk**, or both | Worker: up to **1 year** or **5,000 Tk**; Union: **2 years** or **10,000 Tk** |

### Consequences of ULPs on Employers and Employees (2023 Q.4(b))

- **On Employers:**
  1. *Criminal Penalties:* Fines and up to 2 years prison under Section 291(1).
  2. *Operational Loss:* Wildcat strikes, line halts, missed shipments, and penalty fees.
  3. *Lost Export Orders:* Global buyers cancel purchase orders over compliance breaches.
  4. *Technician Turnover:* Toxic anti-union culture drives away skilled staff, raising rehiring costs.
- **On Employees:**
  1. *Livelihood Loss:* Retaliatory dismissal or wage cuts leave workers without income.
  2. *Suppressed Voice:* Bypassing CBA destroys collective power for fair pay and safety.
  3. *Workplace Fear:* Threats, transfers, and surveillance cause chronic mental distress.
  4. *Statutory Sanctions:* Misconduct dismissal under Section 23 and up to 1 year prison under Section 291(2).

---

### Strategies for Trade Unions to Address ULPs (2023 Q.4(c))

1. **Labour Court Action [Sec 291, 214]:** Prosecute offending managers under Section 291(1). Seek worker reinstatement with back pay under Section 33.
2. **Dispute Settlement [Sec 210]:** Serve dispute notice for bipartite talks within 15 days, escalating to conciliation.
3. **Administrative Complaint [Sec 187]:** Report illegal officer transfers to Director of Labour for rapid intervention.
4. **Evidence Dossier:** Keep logs, transfer letters, recordings, and witness statements for court.
5. **Participation Committee [Sec 205]:** Resolve grievances through bipartite committee before escalation.
6. **Buyer & Federation Pressure [Sec 200]:** Report abuses to global brand compliance teams and national federations.
7. **Member Discipline:** Educate members on Section 196 to preserve statutory immunities under Section 197 and Section 198.

## 🎯 3. PYQ & Target Answer Key

### 2023 Final Q.4(b) [04 Marks] · Consequences of ULPs
- **Question:** Evaluate the potential consequences of unfair labor practices on both employers and employees.
- **Answer Blueprint:** Cite Sec 195, 196, and 291. State 4 employer impacts (liability, lost contracts, turnover, losses). State 4 employee impacts (livelihood loss, suppressed voice, fear, dismissal) from §2.

### 2023 Final Q.4(c) [04 Marks] · Strategies for Unions to Address ULPs
- **Question:** Propose strategies that unions can employ to address unfair labor practices effectively.
- **Answer Blueprint:** Present the 7-point strategy from §2. Cover statutory routes (Sec 291, 210, 187). Add organizational routes (evidence logs, Sec 205 committee, buyer pressure, discipline).

### Shoaib Sir Target Question [04 Marks] · Employer and Worker ULPs
- **Question:** Define unfair labour practice and detail statutory prohibitions for employers and workers.
- **Answer Blueprint:** Quote verbatim definition in §1. Detail 12 employer prohibitions under Section 195. Then explain Section 196 prohibitions for workers.

### Model Applied Case Study [04 Marks] · Illegal Strike, Gherao & Coercion
- **Scenario:** Local union demands 35% wage raise without strike notice or secret ballot (`Sec 211`). Workers cut cleanroom electricity, block factory gates, and confine General Manager for 12 hours (gherao) until he signs agreement. They threaten non-striking technicians with assault.
- **1. Facts:** Cleanroom power cut, gates blocked, 12-hour GM confinement, forced agreement signature, assault threats to non-strikers.
- **2. Law & Sections:** BLC 2006 `Sec 196(2)(a), (d), (e), (f)`, `Sec 211`, `Sec 291(2)–(3)`. Also `Sec 23(4)(g)` and Contract Act `Sec 15, 19`.
- **3. Legal Analysis:**
  - Strike without notice or secret ballot is illegal under `Sec 211` and `Sec 196(2)(e)`.
  - Cutting cleanroom power and confining manager violates `Sec 196(2)(d)` (coercing employer).
  - Gate blockade and 12-hour confinement constitute illegal gherao under `Sec 196(2)(f)`.
  - Intimidating non-strikers with assault violates `Sec 196(2)(a)`.
  - Agreement signed under criminal duress/coercion is voidable and legally void (`Contract Act Sec 19`).
- **4. Direct Conclusion:** All actions violate `Sec 196`. Forced agreement is void. Participating workers face up to 1 year prison / 5,000 Tk (`Sec 291(2)`) and dismissal under `Sec 23(4)(g)`. Union leaders face up to 2 years prison / 10,000 Tk (`Sec 291(3)`).

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Penalty Allocation:** Employers face up to 2 years or 10,000 Tk (`Sec 291(1)`). Workers face 1 year or 5,000 Tk (`Sec 291(2)`). Trade unions face 2 years or 10,000 Tk (`Sec 291(3)`).
> - **Protected Officers:** Section 195(k) specifically protects 4 officers from transfer: President, General Secretary, Organizing Secretary, Treasurer (`Sec 187`).
> - **Strike-Breakers:** Employers cannot hire replacement workers during lawful strikes under Section 195(h) without Arbitrator approval.
> - **Case Framework:** For scenarios, always structure as: 1. Facts -> 2. Law & Section -> 3. Legal Analysis -> 4. Direct Conclusion.
