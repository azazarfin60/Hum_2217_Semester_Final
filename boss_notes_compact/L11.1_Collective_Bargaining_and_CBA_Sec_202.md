# L11.1: Collective Bargaining and CBA (Section 202)

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · Shoaib Islam Sir · 🔴 Essential
> - **Estimated Read Time:** ~4 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2023 Final Q.4(a) (02m), 2024 Final Q.8(a) Part 2 (01m), Shoaib Sir Applied Lock (04m)
> - **Scoring Rule:** State exact thresholds (1/3 workers, 120 days, 10% penalty) and cite Section 202(24)(a) to (e).

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **Collective Bargaining [SI Sir Notes · Chapter 4]:** "Collective bargaining is the process in which working people, through their unions, negotiate contracts with their employers to determine their terms of employment, including pay, benefits, hours, leave, job health and safety policies, ways to balance work and family, and more." *(SI Sir Class Notes · Chapter 4)*

*Plain meaning:* Workers team up through their union to sit down and talk with management. Together, they agree on fair wages, work hours, leave, and safety rules that both sides must follow.

> **Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA) [BLC 2006, Sec 2(52) & Sec 202(1)]:** "A collective bargaining agent is a registered trade union elected by secret ballot. Where there is only one registered trade union in an establishment that trade union shall be deemed to be the collective bargaining agent for such establishment." *(BLC 2006, Section 202(1) & Section 2(52))*

*Plain meaning:* The single official trade union chosen to speak for all workers in the factory. It alone has the legal power to negotiate pay and rules with the boss.

---

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Core Features and Importance of Collective Bargaining

1. **Collective Action:** Workers unite into one voice. One worker alone is vulnerable, but a united union has real bargaining strength.
2. **Bipartite Dialogue (Direct Two-Sided Talks):** The employer and union talk directly across the table. No court or outside judge interferes.
3. **Continuous Process:** The work does not stop when the contract is signed. Both sides keep talking to handle daily problems and complaints.
4. **Mutual Compromise (Give and Take):** Both sides meet in the middle. Workers get fair wages, while the factory protects its profits.
5. **Legally Binding Settlement:** Talks end with a formal written agreement. Both the company and every worker must obey it by law.

**Why Collective Bargaining Matters in Industry:**

- **Prevents Unfair Labour Practices:** It creates clear pay scales and uniform rules. This stops bosses from playing favorites.
- **Maintains Industrial Peace:** It settles worker complaints peacefully at the meeting table. This stops costly strikes and factory shutdowns.
- **Boosts Factory Productivity:** Workers feel heard and respected. High morale leads to better work, fewer mistakes, and less employee turnover.

---

### Determination of CBA [BLC Section 202]

The Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 sets clear rules to certify a single Collective Bargaining Agent:

1. **When There Is Only One Registered Union [Sec 202(1), (5)]:**
   - The Director of Labour declares this union as the CBA automatically.
   - **One-Third Rule:** The union must have at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workers as paid members.
   - If its membership is below one-third of the workforce, it cannot act as the CBA.

2. **When There Are Multiple Registered Unions [Sec 202(2)–(22)]:**
   - **Apply for a Secret Ballot:** Any registered union or the employer can apply to the Director of Labour.
   - **120-Day Vote Window (`Sec 202(2)`):** The Director of Labour must hold a secret ballot within **120 days** of receiving the application. Alternatively, the unions can agree on an Election Commissioner under the 2013 amendment.
   - **15-Day Contest Notice (`Sec 202(3)`):** The Director sends a written notice to all registered unions. Unions must reply within **15 days** if they want to run in the election.
   - **3-Month Voter Rule (`Sec 202(6)`):** The employer provides a worker list. Workers must have at least **3 months** of continuous service to vote. Casual and substitute workers cannot vote.
   - **7-Day Voter List (`Sec 202(11)`):** Contesting unions receive the final certified voter list at least **7 days** before voting day.
   - **45-Meter Quiet Zone (`Sec 202(14)`):** No campaigning, speeches, or rallying are allowed within **45 meters** of any polling booth.
   - **Winning Threshold (`Sec 202(15)(e)`):** To win, a union must get the highest number of votes. Also, its votes must equal at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workers in the factory. Winning a simple majority of cast votes is not enough.
   - **10% Cancellation Penalty (`Sec 202(22)`):** Any contesting union that wins less than **10%** of the cast votes loses its registration automatically. Its trade union status is cancelled.

3. **Term of Office (Tenure) [Sec 202(16)]:**
   - The winning CBA holds office for **2 years** from the date of declaration. For a group of establishments, the term is **3 years**.
   - No new election application is allowed until 150 to 120 days before this term expires (`Sec 202(17)`).

> [!tip] Memory Hook: CBA Magic Numbers
> **120d** (run ballot) $\rightarrow$ **15d** (join race) $\rightarrow$ **3m** (service to vote) $\rightarrow$ **7d** (voter roll) $\rightarrow$ **45m** (quiet zone) $\rightarrow$ **$\frac{1}{3}$** (win threshold) $\rightarrow$ **10%** (loss penalty) $\rightarrow$ **2y** (term of office).

### Exclusive Statutory Powers of CBA [Section 202(24)]

Under **Section 202(24)** of the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, the certified CBA holds five exclusive powers. No ordinary or minority trade union may exercise them:

1. **Bargain with the Employer [Sec 202(24)(a)]:** Exclusive authority to negotiate employment terms, pay scales, work hours, and physical working conditions.
2. **Represent Workers in Legal Proceedings [Sec 202(24)(b)]:** Exclusive right to represent workers in conciliation, arbitration, or Labour Court hearings.
3. **Declare a Lawful Strike [Sec 202(24)(c)]:** Sole authority to issue a strike notice and declare a lawful strike under Chapter XIV.
4. **Nominate Worker Trustees [Sec 202(24)(d)]:** Sole authority to pick worker trustees for welfare committees, provident funds, and the WPPF board.
5. **Conduct Legal Cases [Sec 202(24)(e)]:** Authority to file, conduct, and defend legal cases on behalf of workers before the Labour Court.

**Statutory Auxiliary Entitlements:**

- **Office Room in Factory (`Sec 202(26)`):** The employer must provide a suitable office room on company premises for the CBA.
- **Outside Specialist Help (`Sec 202A`):** The CBA may hire outside legal, economic, or accounting experts to assist during wage negotiations.
- **Check-off System (`Sec 202(25)`):** The employer deducts union dues directly from worker wages if workers give written consent.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: Five Exclusive CBA Powers (B-R-S-N-C)
> - **B** $\rightarrow$ **B**argain with employer on service terms and working conditions [`Sec 202(24)(a)`].
> - **R** $\rightarrow$ **R**epresent workers in conciliation, arbitration, and court proceedings [`Sec 202(24)(b)`].
> - **S** $\rightarrow$ **S**trike notice and declaration of lawful strike [`Sec 202(24)(c)`].
> - **N** $\rightarrow$ **N**ominate worker trustees to provident and WPPF boards [`Sec 202(24)(d)`].
> - **C** $\rightarrow$ **C**onduct legal proceedings before the Labour Court for workers [`Sec 202(24)(e)`].

### Certified CBA vs Ordinary Registered Union

| Legal Parameter | Certified CBA (`Sec 202`) | Ordinary Registered Union (`Sec 176–194`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raise Industrial Dispute (`Sec 209`)** | Sole worker authority to raise disputes | No legal standing (any dispute action is void) |
| **Direct Negotiation (`Sec 202(24)(a)`)** | Exclusive legal right to negotiate | Prohibited (employer cannot negotiate with it) |
| **Call Lawful Strike (`Sec 202(24)(c)`)** | Exclusive right after secret ballot | No authority (any strike called is illegal) |
| **Nominate WPPF Trustees (`Sec 202(24)(d)`)** | Sole authority to nominate worker trustees | No statutory nomination power |
| **Factory Office Room (`Sec 202(26)`)** | Mandatory statutory right | No statutory entitlement |
| **Legal Status (`Sec 194, 197, 198`)** | Body corporate with civil and criminal immunities | Body corporate with civil and criminal immunities |

## 🎯 3. PYQ & Target Answer Key

### 2023 Final Q.4(a) · Collective Bargaining and CBA Definition (02 Marks)

*Verbatim Question:* What is collective bargaining and who is collective bargaining agent?

**Exam Writing Plan:**

1. **Define Collective Bargaining:**
   - Quote the Chapter 4 definition.
   - Explain it as direct talks where workers negotiate pay, hours, and working conditions through their union.
2. **Define Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA):**
   - Cite **Section 2(52)** and **Section 202(1)** of the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006.
   - Identify the CBA as the sole registered union certified to speak for all workers in the factory.
3. **State Core Statutory Conditions:**
   - A single union must have at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of total workers as members.
   - State its statutory term of **2 years** under **Section 202(16)**.
4. **Concluding Takeaway:**
   - Note that only the certified CBA can sign a binding Memorandum of Settlement that covers all workers.

### Shoaib Sir Applied Lock · Minority Union vs Certified CBA Powers (04 Marks)

*Verbatim Question / Target:* In an electronics factory of 900 workers, Union X is the certified CBA with 550 members. Union Y is a registered minority union with 180 members. When the company introduces a new automated testing machine, Union Y serves a 7-day strike notice on the General Manager and demands separate wage talks. Management refuses to meet Union Y. Union Y claims the employer committed an unfair labour practice. Analyze the legal position of Union Y and the employer under Chapter XIII and Chapter XIV of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006.

#### 1. Facts of the Case

- **Establishment:** Electronics manufacturing factory with 900 total workers.
- **Parties:** Union X (certified CBA with 550 members); Union Y (registered minority union with 180 members); Factory Management.
- **Disputed Actions:** Union Y served a 7-day strike notice demanding separate wage talks after automation. Management refused to meet or negotiate with Union Y. Union Y claims the employer committed an unfair labour practice.

#### 2. Applicable Law & Sections

- **BLC 2006 Section 202(24)(a):** Exclusive statutory power of the CBA to negotiate with the employer.
- **BLC 2006 Section 202(24)(c):** Exclusive statutory power of the CBA to issue strike notices and declare strikes.
- **BLC 2006 Section 209:** Strict legal rule that an industrial dispute exists only when raised by the employer or the certified CBA.
- **BLC 2006 Section 196(2)(e):** Worker unfair labour practice for starting, continuing, or instigating an illegal strike.
- **BLC 2006 Section 195(j):** Statutory rule that an employer must reply to dispute letters from the certified CBA only.

#### 3. Legal Analysis

1. **Employer's Lawful Refusal to Negotiate:**
   - Under **Section 202(24)(a)**, only the certified CBA (Union X) has the legal authority to negotiate wages and service rules.
   - Under **Section 195(j)**, an employer commits an unfair labour practice if they fail to reply to letters from the certified CBA.
   - But this legal duty applies **only** to communications from the certified CBA (Union X).
   - An employer has zero obligation to reply to or negotiate with an uncertified minority union like Union Y.
   - In fact, negotiating with Union Y would undermine the certified CBA and violate **Section 202(24)(a)** and **Section 209**.
   - Therefore, management acted completely lawfully and correctly in refusing to meet Union Y.

2. **Illegality of Union Y's Strike Notice:**
   - Under **Section 202(24)(c)** and **Section 209**, an uncertified minority union has zero legal standing to raise a dispute or declare a strike.
   - Under **Section 211(1)**, a lawful strike notice requires two mandatory steps first. The union must first get a Failure Certificate from a Conciliator. Then, at least two-thirds of union members must approve the strike via secret ballot.
   - Union Y skipped every statutory step.
   - Any work stoppage under Union Y's notice is an **illegal strike** under **Section 2(43)**.
   - Calling or joining this strike is a worker unfair labour practice under **Section 196(2)(e)**.
   - Union Y leaders face criminal punishment under **Section 294**, and the union risks cancellation of its registration under **Section 190**.

#### 4. Direct Conclusion

The employer acted entirely lawfully in refusing to negotiate with Union Y. Union Y's strike notice is completely illegal, void, and unenforceable. Only certified CBA Union X has the legal right to raise an industrial dispute. It alone can negotiate compensation adjustments related to automation.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Winning Threshold Trap:** In a secret ballot, the winning union must get the most votes AND at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workers in the factory (`Sec 202(15)(e)`). Winning a simple majority of cast votes is never enough.
> - **10% Penalty Rule:** Any contesting union winning less than **10%** of the cast votes loses its trade union registration automatically under Section 202(22).
> - **Minority Union Standing:** An ordinary registered union has body corporate status (`Sec 194`). But it has zero legal right to negotiate pay terms (`Sec 202(24)(a)`) or raise an industrial dispute (`Sec 209`).
> - **Section Precision:** Always cite the five CBA powers with their exact statutory sub-clauses: **Section 202(24)(a) to (e)**.
