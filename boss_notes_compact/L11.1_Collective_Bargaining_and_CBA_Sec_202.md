# L11.1: Collective Bargaining and CBA (Section 202)

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · Shoaib Islam Sir · 🔴 Essential | **Read Time:** ~4 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2023 Final Q.4(a) (02m), 2024 Final Q.8(a) Part 2 (01m), Shoaib Sir Applied Lock (04m)
> - **Scoring Rule:** State exact thresholds (1/3 workers, 120 days, 10% penalty) and cite Section 202(24)(a)–(e).

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **Collective Bargaining [SI Sir Notes · Chapter 4]:**  
> "Collective bargaining is the process in which working people, through their unions, negotiate contracts with their employers to determine their terms of employment, including pay, benefits, hours, leave, job health and safety policies, ways to balance work and family, and more."
> - *Plain meaning:* Workers negotiate wages, hours, and job conditions together through their union.

> **Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA) [BLC 2006, Sec 2(52) & Sec 202(1)]:**  
> - *Statutory Definition (`Sec 2(52)`):* "a trade union or federation of trade unions of an establishment or group of establishments which is an agent of the workers for collective bargaining in such establishment or group of establishments under Chapter XIII."  
> - *SI Sir Notes / `Sec 202(1)`:* "A collective bargaining agent is a registered trade union elected by secret ballot. Where there is only one registered trade union in an establishment that trade union shall be deemed to be the collective bargaining agent for such establishment."  
> - *Plain meaning:* Sole certified union with exclusive legal authority to negotiate with the employer.

---

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Core Features & Importance of Collective Bargaining

1. **Collective Action:** United worker representation replaces individual weakness.
2. **Bipartite Dialogue:** Direct employer-union talks preserve industrial peace.
3. **Continuous Process:** Ongoing contract administration and grievance handling.
4. **Mutual Compromise:** Balances company profitability with fair wage scales.
5. **Binding Settlement:** Concludes in an enforceable written Memorandum of Settlement.
- **Why It Matters:** Prevents unfair labour practices, stabilizes wages, averts strikes, and boosts factory productivity.

---

### Determination of CBA [BLC Section 202]

1. **Single Union [Sec 202(1), (5)]:** Certified directly if membership $\ge \mathbf{1/3\ (33.33\%)}$ of total workers.
2. **Multiple Unions [Sec 202(2)–(22)]:**
   - **Secret Ballot:** Director of Labour holds poll within **120 days** of application (`Sec 202(2)`) (or unions nominate Election Commissioner under 2013 amendment).
   - **Contest Notice:** Contesting unions reply within **15 days** (`Sec 202(3)`).
   - **Voter Roll:** Workers with $\ge 3$ months service; roll finalized **7 days** before poll (`Sec 202(6), (11)`).
   - **Buffer Zone:** No canvassing within **45 meters** of booths (`Sec 202(14)`).
   - **Winning Rule:** Highest votes AND $\ge \mathbf{1/3}$ of total employed workers (`Sec 202(15)(e)`).
   - **10% Penalty Rule:** Unions securing $< 10\%$ votes lose registration automatically (`Sec 202(22)`).
3. **Tenure [Sec 202(16)]:** Holds office for **2 years** (3 years for group establishments).

> [!tip] Memory Hook: CBA Magic Numbers
> **120d** (ballot) $\rightarrow$ **15d** (contest) $\rightarrow$ **3m** (service) $\rightarrow$ **7d** (roll) $\rightarrow$ **45m** (buffer) $\rightarrow$ **$\frac{1}{3}$** (threshold) $\rightarrow$ **10%** (penalty) $\rightarrow$ **2y** (tenure).

---

### Exclusive Statutory Powers of CBA [Section 202(24)]

Only the certified CBA holds the five exclusive powers under **Section 202(24)**:
1. **Bargain with Employer [Sec 202(24)(a)]:** Sole right to negotiate employment terms, rules, and conditions.
2. **Represent Workers [Sec 202(24)(b)]:** Represent all workers in conciliation, arbitration, or Labour Court.
3. **Declare Strike [Sec 202(24)(c)]:** Sole authority to issue lawful strike notice under Chapter XIV.
4. **Nominate Worker Trustees [Sec 202(24)(d)]:** Nominate worker reps to welfare, PF, and WPPF boards.
5. **Conduct Legal Cases [Sec 202(24)(e)]:** File and conduct court cases on behalf of workers.
- *Auxiliary Rights:* Factory office room (`Sec 202(26)`), expert aid (`Sec 202A`), dues check-off (`Sec 202(25)`).

> [!tip] Memory Hook: Five Exclusive CBA Powers (B-R-S-N-C)
> **B**argain | **R**epresent | **S**trike | **N**ominate | **C**onduct cases [`Sec 202(24)(a)–(e)`].

---

### Certified CBA vs Ordinary Registered Union

| Legal Parameter | Certified CBA (`Sec 202`) | Ordinary Registered Union (`Sec 176–194`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raise Dispute (`Sec 209`)** | Yes, sole worker authority | No (dispute action void in law) |
| **Bipartite Bargaining** | Yes, exclusive (`Sec 202(24)(a)`) | No (bargaining prohibited) |
| **Call Strike (`Sec 202(24)(c)`)** | Yes, exclusive right | No (strike called is illegal) |
| **Nominate WPPF Trustees** | Yes (`Sec 202(24)(d)`) | No statutory nomination authority |
| **Factory Office Room** | Mandatory right (`Sec 202(26)`) | No statutory right |
| **Body Corporate / Immunity** | Yes (`Sec 194, 197, 198`) | Yes (`Sec 194, 197, 198`) |

### Shoaib Sir Applied Lock · Minority Union vs CBA (04 Marks)
*Verbatim Question / Target:* Legal validity of a minority union calling a strike and demanding separate wage talks.

1. **Facts:** Factory has 900 workers. Union X is certified CBA (550 members). Registered minority Union Y (180 members) serves 7-day strike notice demanding separate wage talks. Employer refuses.
2. **Law & Sections:** BLC 2006 `Sec 202(24)(a)` (exclusive bargaining), `Sec 202(24)(c)` (exclusive strike), `Sec 209` (standing), `Sec 196(2)(e)` (illegal strike), `Sec 195(j)` (duty to reply to CBA only).
3. **Legal Analysis:**
   - Under Section 202(24)(a), only certified CBA Union X can negotiate employment terms. Employer cannot negotiate with minority Union Y. Refusal is lawful.
   - Under Section 202(24)(c) and Section 209, non-CBA unions cannot call strikes or raise disputes. Any stoppage under this notice is an illegal strike (`Sec 2(43)`). It is a worker unfair labour practice (`Sec 196(2)(e)` / item 5), penalized under Section 294.
4. **Direct Conclusion:** Employer acted lawfully. Union Y's strike notice is completely void. Only CBA Union X can negotiate terms or call strikes.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Winning Threshold:** Winner needs highest votes AND $\ge \mathbf{1/3}$ of total employed workers (`Sec 202(15)(e)`). Simple majority of cast votes is not enough.
> - **10% Penalty:** Contesting unions securing $< 10\%$ votes face automatic registration cancellation (`Sec 202(22)`).
> - **Minority Union Standing:** Ordinary registered unions have body corporate status (`Sec 194`), but ZERO right to bargain or call a strike (`Sec 202(24)`, `Sec 209`).
> - **Section Precision:** Always cite **Section 202(24)(a) to (e)** specifically for CBA powers.
