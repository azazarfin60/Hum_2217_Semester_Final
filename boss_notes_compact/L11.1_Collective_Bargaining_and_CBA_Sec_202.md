# L11.1: Collective Bargaining and CBA (Section 202)

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · Shoaib Islam Sir · 🔴 Essential
> - **Estimated Read Time:** ~4 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2023 Final Q.4(a) (02m), 2024 Final Q.8(a) Part 2 (01m), Shoaib Sir Applied Lock (04m)
> - **Scoring Rule:** State exact thresholds (1/3 workers, 120 days, 10% penalty) and cite Section 202(24)(a) to (e).

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **Collective Bargaining [SI Sir Notes · Chapter 4]:** "Collective bargaining is the process in which working people, through their unions, negotiate contracts with their employers to determine their terms of employment, including pay, benefits, hours, leave, job health and safety policies, ways to balance work and family, and more." *(SI Sir Class Notes · Chapter 4)*

*Plain meaning:* A negotiation process where workers act collectively through their union to reach a mutually binding agreement with management covering wages, hours, and working conditions.

> **Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA) [BLC 2006, Sec 2(52) & Sec 202(1)]:** "A collective bargaining agent is a registered trade union elected by secret ballot. Where there is only one registered trade union in an establishment that trade union shall be deemed to be the collective bargaining agent for such establishment." *(BLC 2006, Section 202(1) & Section 2(52))*

*Plain meaning:* The single registered trade union certified with the exclusive legal authority to negotiate employment contracts and represent all workers in a factory.

---

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Core Features and Importance of Collective Bargaining

1. **Collective Action:** Workers replace individual vulnerability with united bargaining strength through their certified union.
2. **Bipartite Dialogue:** Direct negotiations proceed between employer and union representatives without outside judicial interference.
3. **Continuous Process:** Bargaining does not end with a contract signature; it continues through daily contract administration and grievance resolution.
4. **Mutual Compromise:** Both parties make concessions, balancing fair living wages with enterprise profitability and operational stability.
5. **Legally Binding Settlement:** Negotiations conclude in an enforceable written Memorandum of Settlement binding all employees and the company.

**Why Collective Bargaining Matters in Industry:**

- **Prevents Unfair Labour Practices:** Establishes objective wage scales and uniform rules, eliminating arbitrary managerial favoritism.
- **Maintains Industrial Peace:** Resolves workplace grievances across a conference table, averting costly strikes and lock-outs.
- **Boosts Factory Productivity:** Increases worker morale, cooperation, and retention by giving employees a direct voice in workplace governance.

---

### Determination of CBA [BLC Section 202]

The Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 establishes specific rules to certify a single Collective Bargaining Agent based on the number of unions present:

1. **Single Registered Trade Union [Sec 202(1), (5)]:**
   - The union is automatically declared the CBA by the Director of Labour.
   - **Mandatory Threshold:** The union must have at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workers employed in the establishment as paid members.
   - If its membership falls below one-third of the workforce, it cannot act as the CBA.

2. **Multiple Registered Trade Unions [Sec 202(2)–(22)]:**
   - **Application for Ballot:** Any registered trade union or the employer may apply to the Director of Labour, who must conduct a secret ballot within **120 days** of receiving the application (`Sec 202(2)`). Alternatively, unions may nominate an agreed Election Commissioner under the 2013 amendment.
   - **Contest Notice:** The Director of Labour issues written notice to all registered unions, requiring a formal reply within **15 days** to participate in the contest (`Sec 202(3)`).
   - **Verified Voter Roll:** The employer submits a verified list of workers with at least **3 months** of continuous service (excluding casual and substitute workers). The final certified roll is delivered to contesting unions at least **7 days** before polling day (`Sec 202(6), (11)`).
   - **Polling Buffer Zone:** Canvassing and electioneering are strictly prohibited within **45 meters** of any polling station (`Sec 202(14)`).
   - **Winning Threshold:** The winning union must secure the highest number of votes, provided its total votes equal at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workforce in the factory (`Sec 202(15)(e)`). A simple plurality or majority of votes cast is legally insufficient.
   - **Automatic Cancellation Penalty:** Any contesting union that receives less than **10%** of the total votes cast loses its trade union registration automatically (`Sec 202(22)`).

3. **Statutory Tenure [Sec 202(16)]:**
   - The certified CBA holds office for a fixed term of **2 years** from the date of declaration (or **3 years** in the case of a group of establishments).
   - No fresh election application may be entertained until 150 to 120 days before this statutory tenure expires (`Sec 202(17)`).

> [!tip] Memory Hook: CBA Magic Numbers
> **120d** (ballot) $\rightarrow$ **15d** (contest) $\rightarrow$ **3m** (service) $\rightarrow$ **7d** (roll) $\rightarrow$ **45m** (buffer) $\rightarrow$ **$\frac{1}{3}$** (threshold) $\rightarrow$ **10%** (penalty) $\rightarrow$ **2y** (tenure).

### Exclusive Statutory Powers of CBA [Section 202(24)]

Under **Section 202(24)** of the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, the certified CBA holds five exclusive statutory powers that no ordinary or minority trade union may exercise:

1. **Bargain with the Employer [Sec 202(24)(a)]:** Exclusive authority to negotiate terms of employment, service rules, remuneration, and the physical working environment.
2. **Represent Workers in Proceedings [Sec 202(24)(b)]:** Exclusive right to represent all workers or any individual worker in conciliation, arbitration, or Labour Court proceedings.
3. **Declare Lawful Strike [Sec 202(24)(c)]:** Sole authority to issue a strike notice and declare a lawful strike under Chapter XIV.
4. **Nominate Worker Trustees [Sec 202(24)(d)]:** Sole authority to nominate worker representatives to factory welfare committees, provident fund boards, and the Workers' Participation Fund (WPPF) Board of Trustees.
5. **Conduct Legal Cases [Sec 202(24)(e)]:** Authority to file, conduct, and defend legal suits and proceedings on behalf of workers.

**Statutory Auxiliary Entitlements:**

- **Office Room Allotment (`Sec 202(26)`):** The employer is legally obligated to provide a suitable office room within the establishment premises for the CBA.
- **Specialist Assistance (`Sec 202A`):** The CBA may engage outside economic, accounting, or legal specialists to advise and assist during collective bargaining sessions.
- **Check-off System (`Sec 202(25)`):** Deducting union subscription dues directly from worker wages upon individual authorization.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: Five Exclusive CBA Powers (B-R-S-N-C)
> - **B** $\rightarrow$ **B**argain with employer on service terms and working conditions [`Sec 202(24)(a)`].
> - **R** $\rightarrow$ **R**epresent workers in conciliation, arbitration, and court proceedings [`Sec 202(24)(b)`].
> - **S** $\rightarrow$ **S**trike notice and declaration of lawful strike [`Sec 202(24)(c)`].
> - **N** $\rightarrow$ **N**ominate worker trustees to provident and WPPF boards [`Sec 202(24)(d)`].
> - **C** $\rightarrow$ **C**onduct legal proceedings before the Labour Court for workers [`Sec 202(24)(e)`].

### Certified CBA vs Ordinary Registered Union

| Legal Parameter | Certified CBA (`Sec 202`) | Ordinary Registered Union (`Sec 176–194`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raise Industrial Dispute (`Sec 209`)** | Sole worker authority to raise disputes | No legal standing (any dispute action is void) |
| **Bipartite Negotiation (`Sec 202(24)(a)`)** | Exclusive statutory right to negotiate | Prohibited (employer cannot negotiate with it) |
| **Call Lawful Strike (`Sec 202(24)(c)`)** | Exclusive right after secret ballot | No authority (any strike called is illegal) |
| **Nominate WPPF Trustees (`Sec 202(24)(d)`)** | Sole authority to nominate trustees | No statutory nomination power |
| **Factory Office Room (`Sec 202(26)`)** | Mandatory statutory right | No statutory entitlement |
| **Legal Status (`Sec 194, 197, 198`)** | Body corporate with civil and criminal immunities | Body corporate with civil and criminal immunities |

## 🎯 3. PYQ & Target Answer Key

### 2023 Final Q.4(a) · Collective Bargaining and CBA Definition (02 Marks)

*Verbatim Question:* What is collective bargaining and who is collective bargaining agent?

**Exam Writing Plan:**

1. **Define Collective Bargaining:** Quote the verbatim definition from Section 1, explaining it as the negotiation process where workers negotiate employment terms, wages, and working hours collectively through their trade union.
2. **Define Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA):** Cite **Section 2(52)** and **Section 202(1)** of the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, identifying the CBA as the sole registered trade union certified by secret ballot or statutory declaration to represent all workers.
3. **Core Statutory Conditions:** State that a single registered union must represent at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workforce to be declared CBA, and note its statutory tenure of **2 years** under **Section 202(16)**.
4. **Concluding Takeaway:** Emphasize that only the certified CBA holds the exclusive legal authority to sign an enforceable Memorandum of Settlement binding the entire establishment.

### 2024 Final Q.8(a) (Part 2) · Collective Bargaining Definition (01 Mark)

*Verbatim Question:* Define Industrial dispute and collective bargaining.

**Exam Writing Plan:**

1. **Statutory Quote & Meaning:** Define collective bargaining using the Chapter 4 definition as a bipartite negotiation process between the employer and the workers' certified trade union.
2. **Essential Scope:** Specify that it governs employment contracts, remuneration, working hours, occupational safety policies, and leave conditions.
3. **Concluding Takeaway:** State that the process legally concludes upon the execution of a written, binding Memorandum of Settlement under Section 210(3).

### Shoaib Sir Applied Lock · Minority Union vs Certified CBA Powers (04 Marks)

*Verbatim Question / Target:* In an electronics factory of 900 workers, Union X is the certified CBA with 550 members. Union Y is a registered minority union with 180 members. When the company introduces a new automated testing machine, Union Y serves a 7-day strike notice on the General Manager and demands separate wage talks. Management refuses to meet Union Y. Union Y claims the employer committed an unfair labour practice. Analyze the legal position of Union Y and the employer under Chapter XIII and Chapter XIV of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006.

#### 1. Facts of the Case

- **Establishment:** Electronics manufacturing factory with 900 total workers.
- **Parties:** Union X (certified CBA with 550 members); Union Y (registered minority union with 180 members); Factory Management.
- **Disputed Actions:** Union Y served a 7-day strike notice demanding separate wage talks following automation. Management refused to negotiate. Union Y alleges an employer unfair labour practice.

#### 2. Applicable Law & Sections

- **BLC 2006 Section 202(24)(a):** Exclusive statutory authority of the CBA to negotiate with the employer.
- **BLC 2006 Section 202(24)(c):** Exclusive statutory authority of the CBA to issue strike notices and declare strikes.
- **BLC 2006 Section 209:** Strict legal rule that an industrial dispute exists only when raised by the employer or the certified CBA.
- **BLC 2006 Section 196(2)(e):** Worker unfair labour practice for commencing, continuing, or instigating an illegal strike.
- **BLC 2006 Section 195(j):** Statutory obligation of the employer to reply to communications regarding industrial disputes exclusively from the certified CBA.

#### 3. Legal Analysis

1. **Employer's Lawful Refusal to Negotiate:**
   - Under **Section 202(24)(a)**, only the certified CBA (Union X) holds the exclusive legal standing to bargain on wages and service rules.
   - Under **Section 195(j)**, it is an employer unfair labour practice to fail to reply to communications made by the certified Collective Bargaining Agent. However, this statutory duty applies exclusively to communications from the certified CBA. An employer is under no obligation to reply to or negotiate with an uncertified minority union like Union Y, and doing so would undermine the collective bargaining framework under **Section 202(24)(a)** and **Section 209**. Management's refusal to meet Union Y is entirely lawful and mandatory.
2. **Illegality of Union Y's Strike Notice:**
   - Under **Section 202(24)(c)** and **Section 209**, an uncertified minority union has zero legal standing to raise an industrial dispute or declare a strike.
   - Under **Section 211(1)**, a lawful strike notice requires a prior Failure Certificate from the Conciliator and approval by two-thirds of union members via secret ballot. Union Y bypassed every statutory step.
   - Any work stoppage pursuant to Union Y's notice is an **illegal strike** under **Section 2(43)** and constitutes a worker unfair labour practice under **Section 196(2)(e)**. Union Y officers face criminal penalties under **Section 294**, and the union risks cancellation of its registration under **Section 190**.

#### 4. Direct Conclusion

The employer acted entirely lawfully in refusing to negotiate with Union Y. Union Y's strike notice is completely illegal, void, and unenforceable. Only certified CBA Union X is entitled to raise an industrial dispute or negotiate compensation adjustments related to automation.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Winning Threshold Trap:** The winning union in a secret ballot election must secure the highest votes AND at least **one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$)** of the total workers employed in the factory (`Sec 202(15)(e)`). A simple majority of cast votes is never sufficient.
> - **10% Penalty Rule:** Any contesting union securing less than **10%** of the total votes cast faces automatic cancellation of its registration under Section 202(22).
> - **Minority Union Standing:** While an ordinary registered union enjoys body corporate status (`Sec 194`), it has zero statutory authority to negotiate employment terms (`Sec 202(24)(a)`) or raise an industrial dispute (`Sec 209`).
> - **Section Precision:** Always cite the five CBA powers with their exact statutory sub-clauses: **Section 202(24)(a) to (e)**.
