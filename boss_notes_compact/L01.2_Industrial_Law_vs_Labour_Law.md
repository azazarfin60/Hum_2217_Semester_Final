# L01.2: Industrial Law vs. Labour Law

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · Shoaib Islam Sir · High Priority | **Read Time:** ~3 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2023 Final Q.2(a) (02m) · Ch. 1 Core High-Yield
> - **Scoring Rule:** State verbatim definitions, two-fold objective, and structured 7-point difference table.

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

1. **Industrial Law:**
   > "Industrial law is used to denote laws passed for the purpose of regulating the conditions under which work is carried out in factories and other establishments and the relationship between employers and employees." (SI Sir Notes & Slides, Chapter 1)
   - *Plain meaning:* Rules governing factory physical conditions, plant safety, and employer-employee relations.

2. **Labour Law:**
   > "Labor law is designed to control and govern the continuous process by which workers and employers decide the terms and conditions of employment. It is a legal framework that governs the rights and responsibilities of employers and employees in the workplace." (SI Sir Notes & Slides, Chapter 1)
   - *Plain meaning:* Legal framework setting employment terms, wages, hours, and worker rights.

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### A. Industrial Law Scope and Two-Fold Objective

Industrial legislation focuses on business operations, factory systems, and production plants. It has a two-fold objective:

1. **Preservation of Health, Safety, and Welfare:** Forces factory owners to maintain machine guards, fire alarms, clean air, and sanitation.
2. **Maintenance of Good Industrial Relations:** Provides formal legal channels to settle disputes peacefully and prevent production halts.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: H-S-W-R Objective
> Remember **H-S-W-R**: **H**ealth, **S**afety, **W**elfare, and Good **R**elations.

### B. Labour Law Scope (Worker Rights Protection)

Labour law is worker-centered. It balances unequal bargaining power across four main areas:

1. **Terms of Employment:** Regulates appointment letters, identity cards, service records, and probation.
2. **Wages and Working Hours:** Enforces minimum wages, overtime compensation, 8-hour daily limits, and weekly rest.
3. **Leave and Maternity Rights:** Guarantees annual leave, sick leave, festival holidays, and 16 weeks paid maternity benefit.
4. **Collective Rights:** Protects union formation and collective bargaining through an elected CBA.

> [!tip] Memory Hook: Factory vs. Worker
> - **Industrial Law** = Regulates the **Factory / Enterprise** (machines, permits, processes).
> - **Labour Law** = Protects the **Worker** (wages, hours, leave, fair treatment).

### C. Full Comparison: Industrial Law vs. Labour Law

| Comparison Parameter | Industrial Law | Labour Law |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Primary Definition** | Laws regulating conditions of work in factories and enterprise relationships. | Laws controlling the continuous process of setting terms of employment. |
| **2. Core Subject** | Relates to the **Industry**, factory premises, and operational processes. | Relates to **Workers**, employment terms, and trade unions. |
| **3. Main Objective** | Preserves worker safety and maintains peaceful industrial relations. | Prevents worker exploitation and balances unequal bargaining power. |
| **4. Scope of Coverage** | Factory permits, machine safety, environment, and enterprise governance. | Wages, working hours, rest intervals, leave, and maternity benefits. |
| **5. Target Unit** | Entire industrial establishments, production plants, and machinery. | Individual workers, trade union members, and employers. |
| **6. Primary Statute (BD)** | Factory safety rules, environmental acts, and commercial regulations. | Centered on **The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (Act XLII of 2006)**. |
| **7. Economic Role** | Maintains continuous production and prevents factory disruption. | Secures economic justice, fair compensation, and worker social security. |

---

### Expected Short Note (02 Marks)
**Question:** State the two-fold objective of industrial legislation.

**Answer Blueprint:**
State the two objectives from §2 Block A: (1) Health, safety, and welfare; (2) Good industrial relations (`H-S-W-R`).

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Not Synonyms:** Never treat them as identical. Industrial law governs factories and machines; Labour law governs worker rights and terms.
> - **Two-Fold Objective:** Omitting either health/safety/welfare or good relations drops a 2-mark answer to 1 mark.
> - **Spelling Consistency:** Both "Labor Law" and "Labour Law" are valid. Keep spelling consistent throughout your script.
