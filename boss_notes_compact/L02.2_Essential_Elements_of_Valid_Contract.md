# L02.2: Essential Elements of a Valid Contract

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A · Shoaib Islam Sir · 🔴 Critical | **Read Time:** ~4 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2023 Final Q.3(b) (04m), 2025 Law-CT1 Q.1 (10m)
> - **Scoring Rule:** State Sec 10, all 10 elements with statutory sections, and cite *Balfour* and *Mohori Bibee*.

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **Contract [Sec 2(h)]:** "An agreement enforceable by law is a contract." (Contract Act, 1872)
- *Plain meaning:* A promise that courts will enforce if breached.

> **Enforceable Contracts [Sec 10]:** "All agreements are contracts if they are made by the free consent of parties competent to contract, for a lawful consideration and with a lawful object, and are not hereby expressly declared to be void." (Contract Act, 1872)
- *Plain meaning:* An agreement becomes a contract only when passing all statutory tests.

> **Competent Parties [Sec 11]:** "Every person is competent to contract who is of the age of majority... of sound mind, and is not disqualified from contracting by any law..." (Contract Act, 1872)
- *Plain meaning:* Sane adults not barred by law can make contracts.

> **Free Consent [Sec 14]:** "Consent is said to be free when it is not caused by (1) coercion... (2) undue influence... (3) fraud... (4) misrepresentation... or (5) mistake..." (Contract Act, 1872)
- *Plain meaning:* Genuine consent given without threats, pressure, deception, or mutual error.

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Foundational Formula & Core Maxim

$$
\text{Valid Contract} = \text{Agreement [Sec 2(e)]} + \text{Legal Enforceability [Sec 10]}
$$

- *"All contracts are agreements, but all agreements are not contracts."*
  - Every contract begins as an agreement formed by exchanged promises [Sec 2(e)].
  - But agreements become enforceable contracts only if they meet all Sec 10 conditions. Social or illegal promises remain mere agreements.

### The 10 Essential Elements of a Valid Contract

1. **Lawful Offer and Acceptance [Sec 2(a), 2(b), 7]:** Requires a lawful offer and unconditional acceptance (*consensus ad idem*). *Ex:* Offering PCB design for Tk 20,000; client accepts unconditionally.
2. **Legal Intention:** Parties must intend binding legal duties. Presumed in business; absent in domestic promises (*Balfour v. Balfour*).
3. **Lawful Consideration [Sec 2(d), 25]:** Requires reciprocal value (*quid pro quo*). Gratuitous promises are void under Sec 25. *Ex:* Paying Tk 50,000 for coding.
4. **Capacity of Parties [Sec 11, 12]:** Parties must be 18+, sane, and legally qualified. Minor agreements are void *ab initio* (*Mohori Bibee*).
5. **Free Consent [Sec 13, 14]:** Requires consent free from coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, or mistake (Sec 15–22). Flawed consent creates voidable contracts (Sec 19).
6. **Lawful Object [Sec 23]:** Purpose must not be unlawful, fraudulent, harmful, or contrary to public policy. *Ex:* Deals to hack servers are void.
7. **Certainty of Terms [Sec 29]:** Terms must be clear. Promising "some chips at a good price" is void for uncertainty.
8. **Possibility of Performance [Sec 56(1)]:** Acts must be physically and legally possible. Promising treasure by magic is void.
9. **Not Expressly Declared Void [Sec 26–30]:** Must avoid statutory bars: marriage (26), trade (27), legal proceedings (28), uncertainty (29), or wagering (30).
10. **Legal Formalities:** Specific laws require written or registered deeds (e.g. land transfers under Registration Act 1908).

> [!tip] Memory Hook: The 10 Elements & Statutory Vices
> - **10-Point Story:** *"An **Offer** with **Intent**, backed by **Cash** and **Capacity**, with **Free Consent** and **Lawful Aim**, makes a **Certain** and **Possible** deal, **Not Void**, in **Formal** writing."*
> - **Free Consent Vices (C-U-F-M-M):** **C**oercion (`Sec 15`), **U**ndue Influence (`Sec 16`), **F**raud (`Sec 17`), **M**isrepresentation (`Sec 18`), **M**istake (`Sec 20–22`).
> - **5 Expressly Void Agreements (M-T-L-U-W):** `Sec 26` **M**arriage, `Sec 27` **T**rade, `Sec 28` **L**egal proceedings, `Sec 29` **U**ncertainty, `Sec 30` **W**agering.

## 🎯 3. PYQ & Target Answer Key

### 2023 Final Q.3(b) · Enforceability Conditions (04 Marks)
*(Note: Exam paper misprinted "contact" for "contract".)*
- **Verbatim Question:** "Summarizes the conditions that must be satisfied for a contact to be legally enforceable." [04m]
- **Answer Blueprint:**
  1. Open with verbatim **Section 10** from §1.
  2. State all 10 essential elements from §2 in crisp bullets.
  3. Cite *Balfour v. Balfour* (intent) and *Mohori Bibee* (capacity).

### 2025 Law-CT1 Q.1 · Role of Essential Elements in Enforceability (10 Marks)
- **Verbatim Question:** "Explain how the essential elements of a valid contract ensure the enforceability of agreements under the Contract Act." [10m]
- **10-Mark Answer Blueprint:**
  - **Introduction:** Quote Sec 2(e), Sec 2(h), core maxim, and the contract formula.
  - **Body (Enforceability Function of Elements):** Present elements 1–10 from §2 as statutory filters:
    - *Assent & Intent (1–2):* Establish *consensus ad idem* and bar casual promises (*Balfour*).
    - *Consideration & Capacity (3–4):* Ensure *quid pro quo* (Sec 25) and shield minors (*Mohori Bibee*).
    - *Consent & Object (5–6):* Give remedies for coercion/fraud (Sec 19) and void illegal goals (Sec 23).
    - *Certainty, Possibility & Statutory Bars (7–9):* Bar vague terms and protect trade freedom (Sec 26–30).
    - *Formalities (10):* Provides authentic documentary proof where mandated by statute.
  - **Conclusion:** These 10 filters ensure courts enforce only genuine, lawful bargains.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Trap 1:** Forgetting to cite **Section 10** as your opening definition loses baseline marks immediately.
> - **Trap 2:** Writing "consent must be free" without naming the 5 statutory vices (**Sec 15 to 22**) drops 1 to 2 marks.
> - **Trap 3:** Confusing void and voidable status. Minor agreements are **void ab initio** (Sec 11), but coercion or fraud makes a contract **voidable** (Sec 19).
