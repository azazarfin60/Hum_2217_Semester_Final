# L10.1: Trade Union Basics, Objectives, and Functions

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Section & Priority:** Section A (CO1 · Shoaib Islam Sir · High Priority) | **Read Time:** ~3 mins
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2024 Final Q.3(a) (02m · Key Objectives); Prescribed Model Questions (Sec 176 Rights & Functions)
> - **Scoring Rule:** List 5 to 6 distinct objectives across economic, safety, and bargaining needs, and cite Section 176 rights (`W-E-F-A-G`).

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **Trade Union [BLC Sec 2(15) / Sec 2(61)]:** "Trade union means an association of workers or employers formed under Chapter XIII, and includes a federation of trade unions." (The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, Section 2(15) / Section 2(61))
- *Plain meaning:* A registered association of workers or employers regulating workplace relations.

> **Trade Union [Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1956]:** "Trade Union means any combination, whether temporary or permanent, formed primarily to regulate the relations between workmen and employers, or between workmen and workmen, or between employers and employers, or for imposing restrictive conditions on the conduct of any trade or business, and includes any Federation of two or more Trade Unions." (SI Sir Notes & Slides, Chapter 4)
- *Plain meaning:* An organization regulating relations between workers and employers, workers and workers, or employers and employers.

> **Industrial Dispute [BLC Sec 2(62)]:** "Industrial dispute means any dispute or difference between employers and employers, or between employers and workers, or between workers and workers, which is connected with the employment or non-employment or the terms of employment or with the conditions of work of any person." (The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, Section 2(62))
- *Plain meaning:* A formal dispute concerning employment terms, work conditions, or worker termination.

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Right to Form Trade Unions & Exclusions [Sec 175–176]

Section 176 of BLC 2006 guarantees five fundamental rights (`W-E-F-A-G`):

1. **Workers' Right [Sec 176(a)]:** Workers can form and join trade unions of their choice without prior permission.
2. **Employers' Right [Sec 176(b)]:** Employers can form trade associations to coordinate industry policies.
3. **Federation and Affiliation [Sec 176(c)]:** Unions can combine into federations and affiliate with international worker bodies (e.g. ILO).
4. **Internal Autonomy [Sec 176(d)]:** Unions freely draft constitutions, elect leaders, and organize their activities.
5. **Mandatory Gender Quota [Sec 176(e)]:** If women form $\ge 20\%$ of staff, the executive committee must include $\ge 10\%$ women.

*Statutory Exclusions [Sec 175]:* Security staff, watch personnel, fire-fighters, and confidential management assistants cannot join worker unions.

---

### Key Objectives of a Trade Union (Answers 2024 Q.3(a))

1. **Better Living Standards:** Secure fair living wages, festival bonuses, and statutory profit-sharing funds (WPPF).
2. **Job Security:** Defend workers against arbitrary dismissals, unlawful retrenchments, and unfair disciplinary penalties.
3. **Collective Bargaining:** Negotiate working hours, shift schedules, overtime rates, and leave policies.
4. **Workplace Health & Safety:** Compel employers to maintain clean drinking water, fire exits, and machine guards.
5. **Industrial Harmony:** Settle grievances peacefully through structured talks to prevent factory shutdowns.
6. **Mutual Welfare:** Provide emergency accident relief, mutual funds, and educational aid for worker families.

---

### Four Core Functional Classes (M-F-S-P)

1. **Militant / Protective (Intramural):** Factory-floor defense: wage bargaining, job security under Chapter II, shift limits, safety checks, and lawful strikes.
2. **Fraternal / Mutual-Aid (Extramural):** Welfare outside work: injury relief funds, worker skill training, housing schemes, and credit co-ops.
3. **Social Functions:** Bridging the communication gap between shop-floor staff and top executives; organizing community health drives.
4. **Political & Civic Functions:** Lobbying for protective labour legislation and nominating delegates to the Minimum Wages Board.

---

### Comparison: Objectives vs. Functions of a Trade Union

| Dimension | Objectives of a Trade Union | Functions of a Trade Union |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | Ultimate goals the union aims to achieve | Daily operational tasks and activities performed |
| **Core Question** | *"What do we want to achieve?"* | *"What actions do we take every day?"* |
| **Scope** | Long-term and outcome-oriented | Ongoing, operational, and process-oriented |
| **Example** | Living wages, job security, and safety | Collective bargaining, credit co-ops, court appeals |

---

### Memory Hooks

> [!tip] Memory Hook: Section 176 Rights (W-E-F-A-G) & Functions (M-F-S-P)
> - **W-E-F-A-G:** **W**orkers form unions (`176(a)`) | **E**mployers form associations (`176(b)`) | **F**ederations & affiliation (`176(c)`) | **A**utonomy in elections (`176(d)`) | **G**ender quota 10% where women $\ge 20\%$ (`176(e)`).
> - **M-F-S-P:** **M**ilitant (wages, safety, strikes) | **F**raternal (mutual aid, training, co-ops) | **S**ocial (bridge worker-boss gap) | **P**olitical (lobbying labour laws, wage boards).

  - *Question:* "Identify the key objectives of a trade union."
  - *Answer Blueprint:* List the 6 concise bullet points from §2 Block 2 (Living standards, job security, collective bargaining, safety, industrial harmony, welfare). Writing these distinct points secures full 2 marks.

- **Model Q2 · Four Categories of Trade Union Functions (04 Marks):**
  - *Answer Blueprint:* Explain the four functional categories (`M-F-S-P`) from §2 Block 3. Add the Objectives vs. Functions comparison table from §2 Block 4.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Confusing Objectives with Functions:** Objectives are desired goals (living wage, job security). Functions are daily actions (bargaining, legal defense, co-ops).
> - **Misquoting Section 176(e) Gender Quotas:** The requirement is 10% female executive seats only if women make up $\ge 20\%$ of the workforce.
> - **Omitting Section 175 Exclusions:** Security guards, fire-fighters, and confidential assistants are barred from joining worker trade unions.
