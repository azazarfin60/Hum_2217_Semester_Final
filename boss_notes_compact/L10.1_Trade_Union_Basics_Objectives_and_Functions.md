# L10.1: Trade Union Basics, Objectives, and Functions

> [!info] ⚡ Exam Snapshot
> - **Curriculum & Weight:** Section A (CO1 · Legal Issues · Shoaib Islam Sir · High Priority)
> - **Estimated Study Time:** ~3 minutes
> - **PYQs Covered:** 2024 Final Question 3(a) (02 Marks · Key Objectives of a Trade Union) and Prescribed Model Questions on Section 176 Rights and Core Functions
> - **Scoring Rule:** List 5 to 6 distinct objectives covering living standards, job security, bargaining, and mutual welfare. Cite the five Section 176 statutory rights (`W-E-F-A-G`).

---

## 📌 1. Verbatim Definitions (For 2-Mark Questions)

> **Trade Union [BLC Section 2(61) / Section 2(15)]:** "Trade union means an association of workers or employers formed under Chapter XIII, and includes a federation of trade unions." *(The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, Section 2(61) / Section 2(15))*

*Plain meaning:* A trade union is an official group of workers or employers registered under labour law. They join together to protect their rights and improve workplace conditions.

> **Trade Union [Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1956]:** "Trade Union means any combination, whether temporary or permanent, formed primarily to regulate the relations between workmen and employers, or between workmen and workmen, or between employers and employers, or for imposing restrictive conditions on the conduct of any trade or business, and includes any Federation of two or more Trade Unions." *(SI Sir Notes & Slides, Chapter 4)*

*Plain meaning:* This wider definition says a trade union can be temporary or permanent. It sets fair rules between workers and bosses, between workers themselves, or between employers. It also sets standard trade terms and includes union federations.

> **Industrial Dispute [BLC Section 2(62)]:** "Industrial dispute means any dispute or difference between employers and employers, or between employers and workers, or between workers and workers, which is connected with the employment or non-employment or the terms of employment or with the conditions of work of any person." *(The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, Section 2(62))*

*Plain meaning:* An industrial dispute is any formal disagreement between bosses and workers. It covers conflicts over hiring, firing, wages, work hours, or workplace safety conditions.

## ⚡ 2. Core Exam Blocks (Write Directly in Exam)

### Right to Form Trade Unions & Exclusions (Sections 175–176)

Section 176 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 guarantees five core rights (`W-E-F-A-G`):

1. **Workers' Right to Form Unions [Section 176(a)] (`W`):** All workers can form and join unions of their own choice. They need no prior permission from employers or the government.
2. **Employers' Right to Form Associations [Section 176(b)] (`E`):** Employers also have the legal right to create their own trade associations.
3. **Right of Federation and Affiliation [Section 176(c)] (`F`):** Registered unions can join together to create larger federations. They can also connect with national or global labor bodies like the International Labour Organization (ILO).
4. **Internal Autonomy [Section 176(d)] (`A`):** Trade unions have full democratic freedom. They can write their own rulebooks, freely elect leaders, and run their own programs.
5. **Gender Quota [Section 176(e)] (`G`):** If female workers reach $\ge 20\%$, the executive committee must have $\ge 10\%$ female members (2013 Amendment).

*Statutory Exclusions under Section 175:* Certain employees are strictly barred by law from joining or forming worker trade unions:

- Security guards and watch-and-ward staff.
- Fire-fighting staff.
- Confidential assistants or personal secretaries to top management.

---

### Key Objectives of a Trade Union (Answers 2024 Q.3(a))

1. **Securing Living Wages:** Win fair wages that match rising living costs. Secure festival bonuses and profit-sharing funds (WPPF).
2. **Protecting Job Security:** Shield workers from unlawful firings, sudden retrenchments, and unfair disciplinary penalties.
3. **Collective Bargaining:** Talk directly with bosses to agree on fair work hours, overtime rates, and paid leave.
4. **Safe and Healthy Workplace:** Demand machine guards, fire alarms, clean drinking water, and clean washrooms.
5. **Industrial Peace:** Settle factory complaints peacefully through direct two-sided talks (`bipartite negotiation`). This prevents strikes and factory shutdowns.
6. **Mutual Welfare Support:** Run emergency relief funds for accidents, sickness benefits, and help with children's school costs.

---

### Four Core Functional Classes (`M-F-S-P`)

1. **Militant or Protective Functions (Intramural · Inside the Factory):**
   - Defend worker rights directly on the shop floor.
   - Bargain for higher wages and fair work shifts.
   - Fight unlawful firings and inspect factory safety.
   - Call lawful strikes if talks break down completely.
2. **Fraternal or Mutual-Aid Functions (Extramural · Outside the Factory):**
   - Provide mutual support outside daily work.
   - Run emergency relief funds for injury or death.
   - Offer skill training classes and literacy courses.
   - Set up discount grocery shops and cheap worker housing.
3. **Social Functions:**
   - Bridge the communication gap between workers and top managers.
   - Run community health camps and blood donation drives in worker neighborhoods.
4. **Political and Civic Functions:**
   - Lobby lawmakers to pass stronger labor laws.
   - Send worker representatives to the Minimum Wages Board to set legal wage floors.

---

### Comparison: Objectives vs. Functions of a Trade Union

| Feature | Objectives of a Trade Union | Functions of a Trade Union |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Meaning** | The final goals and targets the union wants to achieve | The practical daily activities the union performs |
| **Core Question** | *"What do we want to achieve?"* | *"What practical actions do we take every day?"* |
| **Time Horizon** | Long-term and goal-oriented | Ongoing, daily, and action-oriented |
| **Practical Examples** | Fair living wages, job safety, and workplace dignity | Direct talks with bosses, legal defense, and mutual aid funds |

### Memory Hooks

> [!tip] Memory Hook 1: Section 176 Rights (`W-E-F-A-G`)
> - **W** = **W**orkers have the right to form and join unions freely [`Sec 176(a)`].
> - **E** = **E**mployers have the equal right to form associations [`Sec 176(b)`].
> - **F** = **F**ederations can be set up and joined internationally [`Sec 176(c)`].
> - **A** = **A**utonomy to draft constitutions and elect officers freely [`Sec 176(d)`].
> - **G** = **G**ender quota of 10% female executive seats where women make up $\ge 20\%$ of workers [`Sec 176(e)`].

> [!tip] Memory Hook 2: Four Core Functions (`M-F-S-P`)
> - **M** = **M**ilitant / Protective (wages, job defense, safety, strikes inside the factory).
> - **F** = **F**raternal / Mutual-Aid (relief funds, skill training, co-op stores outside work).
> - **S** = **S**ocial (bridging worker-boss communication gaps and community health).
> - **P** = **P**olitical / Civic (lobbying labor laws and Minimum Wages Board representation).

## 🎯 3. PYQ & Target Answer Key

### 2024 Final Q.3(a): Key Objectives of a Trade Union (2 Marks)

> **Appeared:** 2024 Final Exam Question 3(a) · (2 Marks)

**Question:**  
List the key objectives of the trade union.

**Exam-Ready Solution:**

A trade union is an organized group formed to protect worker rights and keep workplace peace. Key objectives include:

1. **Fair Living Wages:** Win wages that keep up with inflation, plus festival bonuses and profit share (WPPF).
2. **Job Security:** Protect workers from unlawful firings, sudden retrenchment, and unfair punishments.
3. **Collective Bargaining:** Agree on fair daily work hours, overtime rates, and paid leave with management.
4. **Health and Safety:** Demand machine guards, fire exits, clean drinking water, and proper toilets.
5. **Workplace Peace:** Settle factory complaints peacefully through direct two-sided talks (`bipartite negotiation`) to avoid strikes.
6. **Mutual Support:** Run emergency relief funds for accidents, sickness benefits, and help with children's education.

*(Tip: Writing 5 to 6 short, clear bullet points ensures full 2 marks.)*

### Model Question 1: Trade Union Definition and Rights under Section 176 (2 Marks)

> **Appeared:** Prescribed Model Question · Shoaib Sir Voice Note Blueprint · (2 Marks)

**Question:**  
Define Trade Union under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. State the statutory rights of workers and employers to form and join trade unions under Section 176.

**Exam Writing Plan:**

1. Quote the Section 2(61) definition from §1. Mention that it includes worker groups, employer groups, and federations.
2. State the five Section 176 rights (`W-E-F-A-G`):
   - Workers can form and join unions freely [`Sec 176(a)`].
   - Employers can form their own associations [`Sec 176(b)`].
   - Federations and international links are allowed [`Sec 176(c)`].
   - Full internal freedom to make rules and elect leaders [`Sec 176(d)`].
   - 10% women's quota in union leadership if women make up $\ge 20\%$ of workers [`Sec 176(e)`].
3. List Section 175 exclusions: security guards, fire-fighting staff, and confidential assistants cannot join worker unions.

---

### Model Question 2: Four Core Categories of Trade Union Functions (4 Marks)

> **Appeared:** Prescribed Model Question · Shoaib Sir Voice Note Blueprint · (4 Marks)

**Question:**  
Explain the core functions of a trade union in modern industry. Group these functions into their recognized categories.

**Exam Writing Plan:**

1. Distinguish functions (daily practical actions) from objectives (long-term goals) using the table in §2.
2. Explain the four functional groups (`M-F-S-P`) from §2:
   - *Militant / Protective (Inside work):* Bargain for wages, defend against illegal firings, and ensure safety.
   - *Fraternal / Mutual-Aid (Outside work):* Run injury relief funds, provide skills training, and open co-op stores.
   - *Social:* Bridge communication gaps between workers and bosses, and run health camps.
   - *Political / Civic:* Lobby lawmakers for better labor laws and join the Minimum Wages Board.
3. Conclude that effective unions balance workplace defense with community welfare.

---

> [!warning] ⚠️ Examiner Traps
> - **Objectives vs. Functions:** Objectives are what the union aims to achieve (like fair wages and job security). Functions are the daily actions it takes (like collective bargaining and running relief funds). Do not mix them up!
> - **The Section 176(e) Gender Quota:** The 10% female executive quota applies only if women make up at least 20% of the workforce. Always write down both numbers: 20% workforce $\to$ 10% executive seats.
> - **Section 175 Excluded Staff:** Security guards, fire-fighters, and confidential assistants cannot join worker unions. Citing this exclusion scores easy bonus marks.
