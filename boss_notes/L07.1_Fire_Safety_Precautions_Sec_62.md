# L07.1: Fire Safety Precautions (Section 62)

> **Section:** Section A (CO1 · Legal Issues · Shoaib Islam Sir) | **Priority:** 🔴 Essential Target (📌 Shoaib Sir's Sole Safety Target for 2026 Final) | **Exam Frequency:** 2023 Q.2(b) (04m), 2025 Law-CT2 Q.1 (10m part), Prescribed Exclusive Safety Section for 2026 Final

> **Sources:** BLC 2006 Chapter VI (`Section 62`), SI Sir Notes (`Chapter-3.md`), Shoaib Sir Voice Note, 2013 BLC Amendments (`Act No. XXX of 2013`)

## 📝 Key Definitions

> **Precaution as to Fire (Section 62(1)):** "Every establishment shall be provided with such means of exit including at least one alternative staircase connecting with every floor at the time of fire and requisite number of fire fighting equipments in every floor as may be prescribed by rules." (The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, Section 62(1))

Every floor must provide two independent escape routes. It must also keep certified firefighting equipment ready for instant use.

> **Unlocked Exit Rule (Section 62(3)):** "In every establishment the doors affording exit from any room shall not be locked or fastened so that they can be easily and immediately opened from inside while any person is within the room..." (Section 62(3))

Exit doors must stay unlocked from the inside while workers are in the room. They must open outward so fleeing crowds can push them open without delay.

> **Mock Fire Drill (Section 62(8)):** "In factories and establishments wherein 50 or more workers or employees are employed, at least once in every 6 months a mock fire-fighting shall be arranged and a book of records in this regard shall be maintained in the prescribed manner by the employer." (Section 62(8), as amended in 2013)

Establishments with 50 or more workers must run practice fire drills twice a year. The employer must log every drill in an official register.

## 1. Alternative Staircase and Fire Equipment (Section 62(1))

Every industrial establishment must provide safe physical escape routes on every level:

1. **Alternative Staircase:** Every floor above the ground floor must have at least one alternative connecting staircase. If smoke or fire blocks the main staircase, workers can reach safety through this secondary route.
2. **Requisite Firefighting Appliances:** Every floor must keep an adequate number of certified firefighting devices. These include portable fire extinguishers, hose reels, sand buckets, and water lines.
3. **Rule Compliance:** The layout, capacity, and placement of stairs and appliances must follow official government rules.

---

## 2. Inspector Compliance Orders and Fire Licenses (Section 62(2))

The Labour Inspector enforces fire standards during factory inspections:

1. **Deficiency Finding:** The Inspector checks whether emergency exits match statutory rules. The Inspector also verifies that firefighting equipment satisfies Fire Service Department license terms.
2. **Written Notice:** If exits or equipment fall short, the Inspector issues a written compliance order to the employer.
3. **Fixed Deadline:** The order specifies the exact safety improvements needed. It sets a strict time limit for the employer to complete all repairs.

---

## 3. Unlocked Outward-Opening Exit Doors (Section 62(3), 3a, 3b, 3c)

Locked doors have caused tragic factory fire deaths in Bangladesh. The statute strictly forbids locked exits:

1. **No Locking While Occupied (`Sec 62(3)`):** Exit doors must never be locked, bolted, or fastened while workers are inside. Any worker must be able to open the door instantly from the inside.
2. **Outward Swing Direction:** All exit doors must open outwards toward the exit path. In an emergency, a panicking crowd presses forward. Inward-opening doors cannot open against crowd pressure. Sliding doors are the only permitted alternative.
3. **Interconnecting Doors:** Doors between two rooms must open toward the nearest building exit.
4. **2013 Statutory Additions (`Sec 62(3a)–(3c)`):**
   - **Section 62(3a):** While work is ongoing, no room exit can be locked, fastened, obstructed, or blocked by any barrier.
   - **Section 62(3b):** Every door must open immediately from the inside outward.
   - **Section 62(3c):** Connecting doors must lead toward the nearest exit and must stay free of locks during work hours.

---

## 4. Distinctive Red Bangla Exit Signage (Section 62(4))

Clear markings guide workers toward safety in heavy smoke and power failure:

1. **Scope of Marking:** Every window, secondary door, or emergency route apart from everyday doors must carry visible markings.
2. **Red Bangla Text:** The markings must use clear red Bangla letters of adequate size (*জরুরী নির্গমন পথ* / *জরুরী বহির্গমন*).
3. **Universal Symbols:** Establishments may also display clear, universally understood emergency signs alongside the Bangla lettering.

---

## 5. Audible Warning Whistle or Fire Alarm (Section 62(5))

Fast evacuation depends on immediate warning signals across noisy machine floors:

1. **Mandatory Alarm:** Every establishment must install a clearly audible warning whistle, siren, or electric fire alarm.
2. **Audible Reach:** The sound must be loud enough to alert every worker on every floor over loud industrial noise.
3. **Instant Activation:** The alarm must sound the moment a fire or dangerous event is detected.

---

## 6. Unobstructed Free Passageways (Section 62(6))

A clear pathway is vital for rapid building evacuation:

1. **Free Access Routes:** Employers must maintain wide, clear passageways in every single room.
2. **Direct Connection:** Every worker must have quick, unhindered access to each fire exit and staircase.
3. **Zero Obstruction:** Hallways, aisles, and door areas must never store raw materials, fabric rolls, scrap, or finished cartons.

---

## 7. Emergency Escape Training for Workers (Section 62(7))

Statutory training ensures workers react quickly without panic during an emergency:

1. **Application Thresholds:** This training is legally mandatory under two specific conditions:
   - More than 10 workers work at any place above the ground floor.
   - Explosive or highly inflammable materials are used or stored on the premises.
2. **Training Duties:** Employers must take effective measures to make every worker familiar with all building escape routes.
3. **Routine Evacuation Drills:** Workers must receive adequate training in the exact evacuation routine to follow during a fire.

---

## 8. Regular Mock Fire Drills and Official Record Book (Section 62(8))

Practice drills build muscle memory for workplace safety:

1. **Workforce Threshold:** This rule applies to all factories and establishments employing 50 or more workers or employees.
2. **Frequency of Drills (Dual Citation Rule):**
   - **Original 2006 Act Text (`Chapter-3.md`):** Mandated a mock fire-fighting drill *"at least once a year"*.
   - **2013 Amendment (`Act No. XXX of 2013`):** Shortened the required interval to *"at least once every 6 months"*.
   - Always cite both the original 2006 yearly rule and the 2013 six-month amendment in your exam answer.
3. **18% Trained Workforce Rule:** Under updated labour rules, at least 18% of the core workforce must complete formal firefighting training.
4. **Mandatory Record Book:** The employer must maintain an official register logging every drill. The record must include dates, participant counts, inspector visits, and safety observations.

## 🏆 Golden Questions (Past Exam Archive & Model Answers)

### 🎯 GQ1 · 2023 Final Q.2(b) · Workplace Safety Rules under Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 (04 Marks)

> **Appeared:** 2023 Final Q.2(b) · Section A (CO1) · (04 Marks)

**Question:**
Briefly discuss the rules regarding the safety of workers in the workplace according to the Bangladesh Labor code 2006.

**Full Answer:**

Workplace safety is governed by **Chapter VI (Sections 61 to 78A)** of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. The Act protects industrial workers from physical collapse, dangerous machinery, and fire disasters:

1. **Safety of Building and Machinery (`Sec 61`):** The Labour Inspector can issue written orders requiring structural repairs. If danger is imminent, the Inspector can prohibit using the building or machine.
2. **Precaution in Case of Fire (`Sec 62`):** Every floor needs an alternative emergency staircase and certified firefighting gear. Exit doors must stay unlocked and open outward. Fire exits require red Bangla signage. Establishments with 50 or more workers must conduct regular mock fire drills.
3. **Fencing of Machinery (`Sec 63`):** Employers must securely fence prime movers and flywheels. Moving gears and transmission shafts also require sturdy guards.
4. **Work on Machinery in Motion (`Sec 64`):** Only a trained adult male worker in tight clothing may service running machines. His name must be entered in the factory register.
5. **Personal Protective Equipment (`Sec 78A`):** Employers must provide personal safety gear free of charge. Workers are legally liable if they fail to wear supplied safety gear.

### 🎯 GQ2 · Model Exam Question · Complete Fire Precaution Rules under Section 62 (10 Marks)

> **Appeared:** Prescribed Master Question · SI Sir Exclusive 2026 Safety Target · (10 Marks)

**Question:**
Detail the rules regarding precaution in case of fire under Section 62 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006.

**Full Answer:**

**Section 62** of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 sets mandatory fire safety precautions for every industrial establishment. The section contains eight detailed subsections:

1. **Alternative Staircase and Firefighting Appliances (`Sec 62(1)`):**
   - Every establishment must provide at least one alternative connecting staircase for every floor.
   - It must maintain a prescribed number of firefighting appliances on every floor, including fire extinguishers and water points.

2. **Inspector Compliance Orders (`Sec 62(2)`):**
   - If an establishment lacks required exits or fire equipment under Fire Service license terms, the Inspector issues a written order.
   - The order specifies required measures and sets a binding deadline for completion.

3. **Unlocked Outward-Opening Doors (`Sec 62(3), 3a, 3b, 3c`):**
   - Exit doors must never be locked or fastened while workers are inside.
   - Any worker must be able to open them immediately from the inside.
   - All exit doors must open outward, unless they are sliding doors.
   - Doors between two rooms must open toward the nearest building exit.
   - Subsections (3a) to (3c) strictly ban any lock, fastening, or physical barrier on escape paths during work hours.

4. **Red Bangla Exit Signage (`Sec 62(4)`):**
   - Every emergency exit, secondary door, or escape window must be clearly marked.
   - Markings must use red Bangla letters of adequate size (*জরুরী নির্গমন পথ*), or other universally recognized emergency signs.

5. **Audible Fire Alarm (`Sec 62(5)`):**
   - Every establishment must install a clearly audible warning whistle, siren, or electric bell.
   - The alarm must alert every worker on every floor immediately in case of fire or danger.

6. **Unobstructed Free Passageways (`Sec 62(6)`):**
   - Employers must maintain wide, clear passageways in every workroom.
   - Every worker must have immediate, free access to all fire exits and staircases at all times.

7. **Worker Escape Training (`Sec 62(7)`):**
   - Escape training is mandatory where more than 10 workers work above the ground floor.
   - It is also mandatory where explosive or highly inflammable materials are used or stored.
   - All workers must become familiar with escape routes and receive training in evacuation routines.

8. **Mock Fire Drills and Record Book (`Sec 62(8)`):**
   - In establishments with 50 or more workers, mock fire drills are mandatory.
   - **Original 2006 Act Text:** Required a mock drill *"at least once a year"*.
   - **2013 Amendment:** Shortened the drill interval to *"at least once every 6 months"*.
   - At least 18% of the core workforce must receive formal firefighting training.
   - The employer must maintain an official record book logging all drill dates and safety details.

### 🎯 GQ3 · Model Case Study · Applied Fire Safety Violations and Legal Liabilities (04 Marks)

> **Appeared:** Prescribed Model Case Study (SI Sir Applied Target) · (04 Marks)

**Question:**
During an unannounced inspection of a four-story electronics assembly plant employing 120 workers, a Labour Inspector discovers three violations. First, the ground-floor exit doors are padlocked from the inside to prevent component theft. Second, cardboard boxes are stacked along the stairwells. Third, the factory has not held a fire drill for fourteen months. Analyze these facts under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 and state the legal consequences.

**Full Answer:**

#### 1. Facts and Issues of the Case
An electronics assembly plant employs 120 workers across four floors. During an inspection, the Labour Inspector finds padlocked exit doors, blocked stairwells, and no fire drills for fourteen months. The legal issue is whether these actions violate statutory fire safety rules. It also asks what actions the Inspector can take.

#### 2. Applicable Law and Sections
- **Section 62(3), (3a):** Prohibition of locked exit doors.
- **Section 62(6):** Mandatory maintenance of free passageways.
- **Section 62(8):** Mandatory mock fire drills every six months for establishments with 50 or more workers.
- **Section 61 & 62(2):** Inspector power to issue orders or prohibit building use.
- **Chapter XIX:** Penalties for non-compliance.

#### 3. Legal Analysis
1. **Padlocked Exit Doors:** Locking doors to prevent theft directly violates Section 62(3) and Section 62(3a). Exit doors must remain unlocked so workers can open them immediately from inside. Commercial security never overrides worker life safety.
2. **Blocked Stairwells:** Stacking cardboard boxes in stairways violates Section 62(6). Free passageways to all escape routes must remain clear at all times.
3. **Lapsed Fire Drills:** The factory employs 120 workers, well above the 50-worker threshold. Under Section 62(8) (as amended in 2013), drills must occur at least once every six months. A fourteen-month lapse violates both the 2013 amendment and the original 2006 yearly rule.
4. **Inspector Enforcement:** Under Section 62(2), the Inspector can serve an urgent written order setting a strict deadline. Locked doors and blocked stairwells may pose imminent danger to human life. In that case, the Inspector can prohibit plant operations under Section 61(2) until repairs are complete.

#### 4. Direct Conclusion
The employer has committed serious violations of Section 62(3), 62(6), and 62(8). The Inspector will issue an urgent compliance order. Continued default exposes the employer to criminal prosecution and fines under Chapter XIX of the Act.

## ⚡ Exam Tips & Common Mistakes

1. **Always Cite Both Drill Frequencies:** Under Section 62(8), cite both drill rules. State the original 2006 text (*at least once a year*). Also state the 2013 amendment (*at least once every 6 months*). Shoaib Sir specifically looks for both.
2. **Mention the 18% Training Requirement:** Under updated safety rules, at least 18% of workers must complete certified firefighting training.
3. **Know the Worker Count Thresholds:** Remember that Section 62(7) applies when more than 10 workers work above the ground floor. Section 62(8) applies to establishments with 50 or more workers.
4. **Explain the Door Swing Rule Clearly:** Emphasize that exit doors must open outward, never inward. Inward doors trap crowds during an emergency rush.
5. **Use the 4-Step Case Study Method:** In any problem scenario, structure your answer into: 1. Facts and Issues, 2. Applicable Law, 3. Legal Analysis, 4. Direct Conclusion.
