## 2. Structured Topic Breakdown

### Module 1: Business and Commercial Law
- **Law of Contract:**
  - Nature and definition of contract (Contract Act, 1872; Section 2(h): *"An agreement enforceable by law"*)
  - Essential elements of a valid contract (Offer and Acceptance, Intention to create legal relation, Lawful consideration, Capacity of parties, Free consent, Lawful object, Certainty, Possibility of performance)
  - Classification of contracts (Valid, Void, Voidable, Unenforceable, Illegal agreements)
- **Consideration:**
  - Definition and legal rules governing consideration (*Quid pro quo*)
  - Doctrine of privity of contract and exceptions
  - Contracts without consideration (Exceptions to *Ex nudo pacto non oritur actio*)
- **Parties Competent to Contract (Capacity of Parties):**
  - Minors (Position and agreements of a minor in law)
  - Persons of unsound mind (Idiots, lunatics, drunken/intoxicated persons)
  - Persons disqualified by law (Alien enemies, insolvents, convicts)
- **Sale of Goods & Hire Purchase:**
  - Sale of Goods Act, 1930: Contract of sale, Distinction between Sale and Agreement to Sell
  - Conditions and Warranties (Express vs. Implied, Doctrine of *Caveat Emptor* and exceptions)
  - Hire and Purchase agreements: Structure, rights, duties, and distinction from normal sale/installment purchase
- **Negotiable Instruments:**
  - Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881: Characteristics and presumption of negotiable instruments
  - Promissory Notes, Bills of Exchange, and Cheques (Features, parties, and distinctions)
  - Endorsement, Holder in Due Course (HDC), Dishonour of cheques and legal remedies

---

### Module 2: Industrial & Labour Law in Bangladesh
- **The Bangladesh Labour Code (BLC 2006 & Amendments):**
  - Scope, objectives, and historical evolution of labour legislation in Bangladesh
  - Fundamental definitions: *Worker*, *Factory*, *Commercial Establishment*, *Employer*, *Manufacturing Process*
- **Employment and Conditions of Service:**
  - Classification of workers (Apprentice, badli, casual, temporary, probationer, permanent)
  - Letter of appointment, service book, identity card
  - Stoppage of work, closure, lay-off, and retrenchment
  - Discharge and dismissal of workers, misconduct procedures, punishment, and enquiry proceedings
  - Termination of employment by notice/payment in lieu of notice
- **Payment of Wages & Working Conditions:**
  - Payment of Wages ordinances and statutory regulations
  - Determination of wage periods, time of payment, and authorized deductions from wages
  - Working hours, working shifts, weekly rest, overtime wages, and annual leave with wages
  - Legislation governing employment in industries, factories, shops, and commercial establishments
- **Trade Union Act & Industrial Relations:**
  - Evolution, constitution, and registration of Trade Unions
  - Rights, duties, and immunities of registered trade unions and union officers
  - Collective Bargaining Agent (CBA): Determination, powers, and functions
  - Settlement of industrial disputes: Negotiation, conciliation, arbitration, and Labour Courts / Labour Appellate Tribunal
  - Lawful and unlawful strikes and lock-outs

---

### Module 3: Industrial Management & Organizational Behavior
- **Principles and Fundamentals of Management:**
  - Definition, nature, and importance of management
  - Efficiency vs. Effectiveness in organizational contexts
  - Evolution of management thought:
    - Classical Administrative Theory (Henri Fayol's 14 Principles of Management)
    - Scientific Management Theory (Frederick W. Taylor's Principles, time and motion study)
- **Management Functions & Skills:**
  - Core management functions: Planning, Organizing, Leading/Directing, Controlling, Staffing
  - Management hierarchy and levels: Top-level, Middle-level, First-line management
  - Essential managerial skills (Robert Katz model): Conceptual, Human, and Technical skills
- **Organizational Structure & Authority:**
  - Authority, Responsibility, and Accountability relationships
  - Delegation of authority and decentralization
  - Span of Control (Narrow vs. Wide span, determining factors, Graicunas' formula)
- **Management Philosophies & Decision Making:**
  - Management by Objectives (MBO): Steps, benefits, and implementation
  - Consultative Management vs. Participative Management (Worker participation in management)
  - Decision-Making: Process, rational decision model, constraints, and group decision making
- **Manpower Motivation:**
  - Concept and significance of motivation in industry
  - Content Theories: Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, Herzberg's Two-Factor (Hygiene-Motivator) Theory, Alderfer's ERG Theory
  - Process & Behavior Theories: McGregor's Theory X and Theory Y, Vroom's Expectancy Theory

---

### Module 4: Human Resources Management (HRM)
- **Manpower Planning:**
  - Human resource planning process, objectives, and significance
  - Forecasting manpower demand and supply; HR job analysis, job description, and job specification
- **Recruitment & Selection:**
  - Internal and external recruitment sources, advantages and trade-offs
  - Systematic selection procedure: Screening, aptitude/technical tests, comprehensive interview, background checks
- **Employee Training & Development:**
  - Training needs assessment (TNA)
  - On-the-job training (Apprenticeship, job rotation, mentoring) vs. Off-the-job training (Lectures, vestibule training, simulation)
- **Performance Appraisal:**
  - Objectives and criteria of employee appraisal
  - Appraisal methods: Graphic rating scales, Management by Objectives (MBO), 360-degree feedback, Critical incident method
- **Wages & Salary Administration:**
  - Principles of sound compensation policy
  - Job evaluation techniques (Ranking, grading, point rating, factor comparison)
  - Wage incentive schemes and non-monetary fringe benefits

---

### Module 5: Production Management & Quantitative Operations
- **Plant Layout:**
  - Definition, objectives, and importance of plant layout
  - Basic layout types:
    - Process (Functional) Layout
    - Product (Line) Layout
    - Fixed-Position Layout
    - Cellular (Group Technology) / Hybrid Layout
  - Layout problem solving: Line balancing techniques, cycle time, theoretical minimum workstations, balance delay
- **Linear Programming (LP):**
  - Mathematical formulation of linear programming models (Objective function, decision variables, constraints)
  - Graphical method for solving 2-variable LP problems (Feasible region, corner-point method, optimal solution)
  - Introduction to Simplex concepts, dual problems, and sensitivity analysis
- **Inventory Control Fundamentals:**
  - Basic inventory definitions, holding costs, ordering/setup costs, shortage costs
  - Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) model derivation:
    $$
    EOQ = \sqrt{\frac{2DS}{H}}
    $$
  - Lead time, safety stock determination under demand variability
  - Re-order Point (ROP) calculation:
    $$
    ROP = (\bar{d} \times L) + SS
    $$

---

### Module 6: Operational Management & Supply Chain Planning
- **Production Systems & Product Life Cycle:**
  - Types of production systems: Job shop, batch production, repetitive/mass production, and continuous process
  - Product/Service Life Cycle (Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline) and operational implications
- **Forecasting Models:**
  - Qualitative forecasting (Delphi method, market research, executive opinion)
  - Quantitative time-series forecasting: Simple moving average, Weighted moving average, Exponential smoothing, Trend projection
- **Bill of Materials (BOM) & Inventory Management:**
  - Single-level and multi-level Bill of Materials (BOM) trees, product structures, and gross-to-net logic
  - Inventory models: Deterministic vs. Probabilistic models
  - ABC Analysis (Pareto 80/20 rule of inventory value), VED analysis, FSN analysis
  - Material coding, standardisation, and cataloguing systems
- **Operations Planning & Scheduling:**
  - Aggregate Planning: Chase strategy, Level capacity strategy, Hybrid strategy
  - Master Production Schedule (MPS)
  - Material Requirements Planning (MRP, closed-loop MRP, MRPII, ERP integration)
  - Capacity Planning: Rough-cut capacity planning (RCCP) and Capacity Requirements Planning (CRP)
  - Operational Scheduling: Priority dispatching rules (FCFS, SPT, EDD, LPT), Johnson's rule for $n$-jobs on 2 machines
- **Facility Location & Layout Algorithms:**
  - Factors affecting industrial facility location decisions
  - Location analysis algorithms: Center-of-Gravity method, Factor-Rating method, Load-Distance analysis, Break-even location analysis
  - Layout optimization techniques: Travel charting, computerized layout algorithms (CRAFT, ALDEP overview)
- **Work Study & Motion Economy:**
  - Method Study: Recording techniques, flow process charts, string diagrams, two-handed operation charts
  - Work Measurement (Time Study): Determining observed time, rating factor, normal time, standard allowance, and standard time

---

### Module 7: Industrial Safety & Risk Management
- **Evolution of Modern Safety Concepts:**
  - Historical perspective: Reactive vs. Proactive safety management
  - Modern system safety, human factors engineering, and behavioral safety
- **Industrial Hazards & Risk Assessment:**
  - Classification of industrial hazards: Physical, chemical, electrical, mechanical, biological, and ergonomic hazards
  - Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment (HIRA), Risk matrix (Severity vs. Likelihood)
  - Hierarchy of Hazard Controls: Elimination, Substitution, Engineering controls, Administrative controls, Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- **Productivity & Occupational Health:**
  - Relationship between safety standards, employee morale, and industrial productivity
  - Worker health and occupational hygiene (Noise, illumination, ventilation, toxic dust, ergonomics)
  - Work-related musculoskeletal disorders (WMSD) and occupational diseases
- **Proactive Safety Management Techniques:**
  - Job Safety Analysis (JSA) / Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)
  - Safety audits, safety inspections, near-miss reporting and investigation
  - Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA), HAZOP (Hazard and Operability study) overview
- **Safety Standards, Regulations & Compliance:**
  - Statutory safety standards and regulations for engineering plants (BLC 2006 Safety Provisions, OSHA standards, ISO 45001)
  - Machine guarding, pressure vessels, boilers, lifting equipment, electrical safety interlocks
- **Fire Safety & Hazardous Materials (HazMat):**
  - Chemistry of fire: Fire triangle and fire tetrahedron; classes of fires (Class A, B, C, D, K)
  - Fire prevention, detection systems (smoke/heat detectors), fire suppression equipment (sprinklers, extinguishers) and emergency evacuation protocols
  - Hazardous Materials (HazMat): Classification, storage, handling, labeling (GHS labels)
  - Safety Data Sheets (SDS / MSDS) and safe disposal of toxic/industrial waste
